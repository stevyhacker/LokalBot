Search your meetings from Raycast

Finding out what was said in a meeting shouldn't mean opening another app. The LokalBot extension, now in the Raycast Store, searches your meetings from Raycast.

October 2, 2026 · 2 min read

Two commands

  • Quick Recall. Type a word and see every meeting where it came up, from titles, summaries, or the transcript itself. Enter reveals the meeting's files in Finder and ⌘Enter copies the matching snippet.
  • Recent Meetings. Lists your last 20 meetings.

Local, like the app

The extension makes no network requests. It reads your local LokalBot library, and only after you allow external agents to read your meeting library in LokalBot's privacy settings.

It needs the LokalBot app installed, which on Mac means Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.

Install

Get LokalBot in the Raycast Store. It's the first LokalBot extension for Raycast, so tell us what's missing or annoying.
