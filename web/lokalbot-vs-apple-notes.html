LokalBot vs Apple Notes

Why not just use Apple Notes? On macOS 27 it records audio into a note, transcribes it on your Mac, and Apple Intelligence writes a summary — free, with nothing to install. For a voice memo or a lecture, that may be all you need. For meetings, the gap is the other side of the call: Notes records your microphone, so with headphones on it doesn't hear Zoom, Teams, or Meet. LokalBot records the meeting app's audio too, turns each call into notes linked to the discussion, and keeps every meeting searchable on your Mac.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Apple Notes if…

Already on your Mac, and good at what it covers.

  • You record lectures, voice notes, or in-person conversations near your Mac's microphone
  • You record phone calls in the Phone app — both sides hear a notice, and the recording lands in Notes
  • You want recordings synced to your iPhone and iPad
  • You'd rather not download several gigabytes of models

Pick LokalBot if…

For calls on Zoom, Teams, or Meet — and what you do after them.

  • You need both sides of a video call recorded, even with headphones on
  • You want decisions and action items you can trace back to the discussion
  • You want to search and ask across all your meetings, not one note at a time
  • You want recordings kept in a local library, with source code you can read

Questions

The short answers.

Can't I record a Zoom call in Notes?

Only your side, unless the other voices reach your microphone through your speakers. Notes on macOS 27 requests microphone access only, so it can't capture another app's audio. LokalBot asks for both: the microphone for you, and macOS audio capture for the meeting app, so the other participants are recorded even through headphones. Either way, tell people you're recording.

Are Apple Intelligence summaries private?

Apple designs them to be. Requests run on your Mac or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers, which Apple says don't keep your data. LokalBot's built-in summaries never leave your Mac, and its source code is public, so you can check. Notes stored in iCloud are end-to-end encrypted only if you turn on Advanced Data Protection.

Does LokalBot replace Notes?

No. Keep Notes for notes, lists, and phone-call recordings. LokalBot is for meetings and recall: it records calls, writes notes you can trace back to the source, and exports them as Markdown when you want them elsewhere.
