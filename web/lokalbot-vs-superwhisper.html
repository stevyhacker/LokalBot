LokalBot vs Superwhisper

Superwhisper started as a dictation app and is now a cross-platform voice tool: custom modes, AI rewrites, meeting recording, and apps for Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android. If voice-to-text is your main job, it goes deeper than LokalBot. LokalBot's dictation is simpler by design — hold ⌥ Space, speak, it types — and it comes inside a free, open-source Mac app that also writes meeting notes, tracks your day, and autocompletes your writing.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Superwhisper if…

Dictation power users are its core audience, and it shows.

  • Dictation is your primary way of writing, all day
  • You want per-app modes and AI rewriting of what you say
  • You dictate on your phone or a Windows PC too

Pick LokalBot if…

Good dictation, plus everything around it — for free.

  • You want dictation plus meeting notes, a day timeline, and autocomplete
  • One model download covers your meetings and your dictation
  • You prefer free and open source over a subscription
  • You want local-by-default behavior you can verify in the code

Questions

The short answers.

Which speech models does LokalBot use?

Qwen3-ASR 1.7B by default, with Parakeet, Granite Speech, and Whisper large-v3 turbo as alternatives, all running on your Mac. Dictation reuses the engine and language you picked for meetings and keeps it warm, so transcription starts as soon as you hold the key.

Does LokalBot keep my dictation audio?

No. By default it records to a scratch file, transcribes it on-device, pastes the text, and deletes the recording. It even pauses your music before it starts listening.

Can I run both apps?

Sure — they use different shortcuts. Some people will want Superwhisper for heavy dictation workflows and LokalBot for meetings and everything else. LokalBot is free, so trying the combination costs nothing.
