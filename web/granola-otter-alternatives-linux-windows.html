Linux and Windows

Is there a Granola or Otter alternative for Linux and Windows?

Yes. Granola has no Linux app, and Otter and Fireflies give Linux users only a web app and a browser extension. Several apps record meetings without a bot on Linux and Windows, but they differ a lot on where your audio is transcribed.

What to check before you choose

  • Where transcription runs by default. Some apps are local only on a Mac, and some recommend their cloud during setup. If audio must stay on your computer, check the setting for meetings, dictation, and file uploads separately.
  • What's free. Speaker labels, meeting detection, longer meetings, and MCP access for AI tools are paid features in some apps.
  • How agents reach your notes. A local MCP server reads the library on your computer. A hosted one needs your notes in the vendor's cloud.
  • Open source or source-available. MIT and GPLv3 let anyone use, change, and share the code; a source-available license can restrict commercial use.
  • Your desktop. On Linux, Wayland limits what any app can do with global shortcuts, typing into other apps, and screen capture. Check what works on your desktop before you rely on it.

FAQ

Questions about this guide

Does Granola work on Linux?

No. Granola's apps are for Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. Its web app can view and edit notes but can't record or transcribe a meeting.

Can I use Otter or Fireflies on Linux?

Only through their web apps and Chrome extensions. Their desktop apps are for Windows and Mac, their notetakers join calls as a bot unless you record from the desktop app or the extension, and transcription happens in their cloud.

Which open-source apps transcribe locally on Linux?

LokalBot and OpenWhispr both transcribe on a Linux computer; with OpenWhispr, choose on-device transcription during setup. Meetily transcribes locally too, but on Linux you build it from source. Anarlog's Linux beta transcribes in the cloud or on a server you run. Screenpipe transcribes locally by default, under a source-available license.
