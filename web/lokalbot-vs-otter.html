LokalBot vs Otter

Otter is one of the best-known AI notetakers: its assistant joins Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, transcribes in the cloud, and shares notes with your team. That reach comes with a participant in your call and your conversations on Otter's servers. LokalBot takes the opposite approach: it captures the call from your Mac's own audio, transcribes and summarizes on the Mac, and needs no account.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Otter if…

The cloud notetaker most teams already know.

  • Your team wants shared, searchable call notes in one workspace
  • You also work on Windows, the web, or your phone
  • A visible assistant in the call suits your meeting culture
  • You want integrations and admin controls out of the box

Pick LokalBot if…

For conversations that shouldn't live on someone else's server.

  • Nothing joins your call and nothing shows up in the participant list
  • Client, patient, or source conversations must stay on your Mac
  • You want no minute caps, no account, and no subscription
  • You want dictation and a day timeline in the same app

Questions

The short answers.

Does LokalBot join my calls like Otter does?

No. Nothing joins the call and nothing appears in the participant list. LokalBot records your microphone and your Mac's system audio, so it works with Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack huddles, or any app that plays sound. You're still responsible for telling participants you're recording.

Is on-device transcription accurate enough?

For most meetings, yes — but test it on your own audio. LokalBot defaults to Qwen3-ASR and lets you switch to Parakeet, Granite Speech, or Whisper in Settings. The benchmarks page shows speed and error rates for the models the project has tested.

What does LokalBot cost after the free tier?

There is no tier. LokalBot is free and open source under GPLv3, with no minute caps, no account, and no subscription.
