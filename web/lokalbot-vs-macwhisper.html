LokalBot vs MacWhisper

MacWhisper is the go-to Mac app for transcribing files: drag in an interview, lecture, or podcast and get text back, on-device. It also records calls without a bot and offers dictation. LokalBot overlaps on meetings and dictation but is built around recall rather than files — meeting notes with local summaries, a searchable library, and a day timeline. LokalBot is free and open source; MacWhisper has a free version and a one-time Pro license.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick MacWhisper if…

The specialist for turning recordings into text.

  • You transcribe interviews, lectures, podcasts, or voice memos you already have
  • You need subtitle files or batch transcription
  • You like paying once for a polished single-purpose tool
  • You want summaries from your own cloud AI keys

Pick LokalBot if…

For meetings and memory, not just transcripts.

  • You want meeting notes with summaries and follow-ups, not only raw text
  • You want to search across meetings and what was on your screen
  • Free and open source matters to you
  • You want summaries from a local model with no keys to manage

Questions

The short answers.

Can LokalBot transcribe an existing audio file?

Not today. LokalBot transcribes the meetings and dictation it records. For files you already have, MacWhisper is the better tool — and the two apps run fine side by side.

Which one is more private?

Both can transcribe entirely on your Mac. The difference is in what surrounds it: LokalBot's summaries run on a built-in local model by default, and its source code is public under GPLv3, so you can check what it does with your audio.

Do they use the same speech models?

Partly. Both can run Whisper-family models on your Mac. LokalBot defaults to Qwen3-ASR and also offers Parakeet and Granite Speech; the benchmarks page compares several of them on the same test set.
