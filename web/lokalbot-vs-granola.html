LokalBot vs Granola

Granola is one of the most polished AI notepads around — bot-free capture, great templates, strong team features, and apps on desktop and phone. The trade-off is that your meetings are processed in the cloud, under an account. LokalBot keeps the bot-free workflow but transcribes and summarizes on your Mac by default: no account, no LokalBot backend, and remote inference only when you approve the server.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Granola if…

It is a genuinely good product — these are real advantages.

  • You want shared folders, team workspaces, and admin controls
  • You take notes on your phone or watch too
  • You prefer AI-polished typed notes over full transcripts
  • You need a vendor with SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA-ready workspaces

Pick LokalBot if…

Built for when the content of your meetings must stay yours.

  • Your meetings can't leave the machine — legal, health, finance, source protection
  • You want no account and no per-seat subscription — it's free
  • You want dictation, a day timeline, and autocomplete in the same app
  • You want source code you — or your security team — can read

Questions

The short answers.

Isn't bot-free capture the differentiator?

Not anymore — Granola and several others skip the meeting bot now. The differentiator is where processing happens. LokalBot transcribes and summarizes on the Mac with its built-in default; if you opt into a remote inference server, it receives the request context.

What does LokalBot send over the network?

Model downloads, update checks, optional Agent Mode setup, and remote inference origins you explicitly approve. Audio stays on the Mac; built-in inference keeps transcript and workday context there too.

Can I try LokalBot next to Granola?

Yes. LokalBot is free — run it on your next few meetings and compare the notes. Neither app puts a bot in your call, so nobody on the call will notice either one.
