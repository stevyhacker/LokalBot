How autocomplete works

Talk instead of typing

Hold ⌥ Space (Alt+Space on Windows and Linux), speak, and release. The words appear at your cursor, transcribed on your computer.

Rewrite what you selected

Select text in any app and press ⌃⇧ W (Ctrl+Alt+Space on Windows and Linux) to fix, shorten, or translate it, or draft a reply from what your meetings say.

Draft the follow-up

Turn a meeting into a draft you review and send. Keep notes in Obsidian or Logseq, and get weekly work logs.

Agent access

Works with your agents.

Claude Code, Codex, or any MCP client can search your meetings through lokalbot-cli or its MCP server.

  • Read-only. Agents can't change your library.
  • Off until you turn it on. Screen memory needs its own permission.
  • Personal Context, only if you share it, under a separate permission.
  • Your agent, your provider. Results go to its model provider, so connect only clients you trust.
# Connect Claude Code over MCP
$ claude mcp add lokalbot -- lokalbot-cli mcp

# Or search from any shell or agent
$ lokalbot-cli search "database decision"
$ lokalbot-cli actions --owner me

Your recordings stay on your computer.

Recording, transcription, and the built-in models run on your computer. You choose what to capture and how long to keep it.

Models and updates need a download. A remote model gets context only after you approve it. Agents and external tools have separate permissions.

System requirements · Setup help and feedback

A few questions.

What does it capture from my screen?

New installs track only app and window activity, and you can turn that off. Screen text and encrypted screenshots are opt-in and deleted after 14 days by default; moments you save stay until you delete them. Excluded apps and sites, password managers, and focused password fields are skipped. Private browser windows are captured like any other, so exclude the browser or site to keep them out.

Does anything leave my computer?

Recordings and built-in processing stay local. The network is for model downloads, update checks, and optional agent setup. A remote model gets context only after you approve its server. Approved agent commands can use the network, and connected CLI or MCP tools have their own data handling. See the privacy policy.

Can I choose my own models?

Yes. Use the built-in models, another downloaded model, Ollama, an OpenAI-compatible server, or, on Mac, Apple Intelligence. Summaries and chat can also run on your ChatGPT or Claude plan, with no API key. Remote options get context only after you approve them. See the transcription model guide and offline setup guide.

Which languages does it support?

Transcription covers 52 languages and dialects by default. Notes follow the meeting's language unless you choose another, and the app is translated into 24 languages. On Mac, autocomplete also works in German, French, Italian, Spanish, and more.

Is LokalBot free?

Yes. It's open source under GPLv3, with no account, subscription, or LokalBot server. See the fact sheet.

What does it run on?

Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 15 or later, plus Windows 10 and 11 (x64) and 64-bit Linux in alpha. Intel Macs aren't supported. See the system requirements.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. LokalBot records your microphone and the meeting's audio on separate tracks. On Windows and Linux the second track is the meeting app where it can be separated, otherwise everything your computer plays. Tell participants you're recording and get their consent. Read how recording works.
