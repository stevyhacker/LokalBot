Pick up where you left off.

What do I still need to do?

Today shows the day's summary and every open action, each linked to the meeting it came from. Each morning the overnight review adds a look back at yesterday.

Where did I save that?

Search the folders you choose by what's in them: documents, PDF pages, pictures, and moments in recordings and video.

Where did the day go?

Retrace your day session by session. Tracking starts with app and window activity; screen text and screenshots are opt-in. If you use Claude Code or Codex, it can name the work by project and show related pull requests and commits.

What's open with Maya?

See what's waiting on you and what you decided together. Name a speaker once, and LokalBot recognizes their voice in later meetings.

Where does this project stand?

Track a project, and LokalBot gathers the meetings, actions, and decisions that name it. Open it for a short note on where it stands, with links back to the meetings behind it.

Not sure what to track? It suggests names that keep coming up in your meetings.

Personal Context

It remembers how you work.

An encrypted list of your facts, preferences, goals, and principles. Tell Ask "Remember I want the agenda the day before," add items in Settings, or let the overnight review pick up facts and preferences from your own words in meetings. Ask uses what it holds, so answers fit your work.

  • Only your own words. It learns from your lines, never other speakers'. The model that reads them stays on your computer unless you approve a server.
  • You see where it came from. Every learned item shows the line behind it. Preferences, and anything from what you typed, wait for you to keep or dismiss them. Facts and goals from meetings are saved directly.
  • Yours to turn off. Learning is on by default, at up to three items a night. One switch in Settings stops it.
  • Private from agents. Agents read only the items you choose to share, under a separate permission.

A hand with the writing, too.

Finish the sentence

Optional autocomplete suggests how your sentence ends, and Tab takes the next word. It reads the message you're answering and, if you let it, what was decided in your meetings. On Mac it also writes in German, French, Italian, Spanish, and more.

How autocomplete works

Talk instead of typing

Hold ⌥ Space (Alt+Space on Windows and Linux), say what you want to write, and release. The words appear at your cursor. Dictation runs on your computer, and your recent dictations are kept so you can copy one again.

Rewrite what you selected

Select text in any app and press ⌃⇧ W (Ctrl+Alt+Space on Windows and Linux) to fix the grammar, shorten it, or translate it. Draft a reply with my memory writes a reply that uses what your meetings actually say.

Draft the follow-up

Turn a meeting into a follow-up draft that you review and send. Also keep a note for every meeting in your Obsidian or Logseq folder and get weekly work logs.

Windows and Linux (alpha)

Not only on Mac.

A native app for Windows 10 and 11 and for Linux has the same pages and reads the same library, so a library moves between computers. Meetings, transcription, Ask, search, day memory, dictation, autocomplete, selection actions, spoken summaries, Files, overnight review, Personal Context, and agent access all work.

It's an alpha: the Windows build isn't code-signed yet, Wayland support is partial, and a few Mac features, such as Gmail in meeting briefs, haven't been ported. On Linux, the .deb, and the .rpm on Fedora, add the LokalBot repository, so apt or dnf keeps it updated. What works and what's missing · Linux guide · Windows guide

Agent access

Works with your agents.

Claude Code, Codex, or any MCP client can search your meetings through the bundled lokalbot-cli or its MCP server. Ask what was decided, who owns an action item, or where a topic came up.

  • Read-only. Agents can search and read your library. They can't change it.
  • Off until you turn it on in Settings. Screen memory needs its own permission.
  • Personal Context, only if you share it. Agents can also read the facts, preferences, goals, and principles you choose to share, under a separate permission.
  • Your agent, your provider. Results go to the agent's model provider, so connect only clients you trust.

Ready to act on a meeting? Open any action item in Claude Code or Codex, and the prompt is already typed in with the task and where it came from, for you to review and send. Separately, optional Agent Mode works on your files with your approval, and it can run on your ChatGPT plan.

# Connect Claude Code over MCP
$ claude mcp add lokalbot -- lokalbot-cli mcp

# Or search from any shell or agent
$ lokalbot-cli search "database decision"
$ lokalbot-cli actions --owner me

Under the hood

A small AI team on your computer.

Each job has its own local model. These are the Mac defaults, about 9.6 GB of model files if you use every role. Windows and Linux run the same jobs with their own local models, about 4 GB by default, on a Vulkan GPU, the CPU, or an NVIDIA GPU with CUDA (experimental).

  • Knows who's talkingNemotron 30.2 GB
  • Turns speech into textQwen3-ASR 1.7B2.47 GB
  • Writes the recap and answersQwen3.5 4B2.74 GB
  • Finishes your sentencesGemma 4 E2B3.85 GB
  • Searches by meaningEmbeddingGemma 20.31 GB
  • Reads screen text, if you opt inApple VisionBuilt into macOS

Privacy

Your recordings stay on your computer.

Recording, transcription, and the built-in models run on your computer. Screen text and screenshots are optional. You choose what to capture and how long to keep it.

Models and updates need a download. If you choose a remote model, the app asks before sending it context. Agent commands and external tools have separate permissions and data handling.

Switching

Coming from another app?

Bring your old recordings with you. Drop an MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, MOV, or other audio or video file onto Meetings, and it becomes a meeting with a transcript, speakers, and notes.

Granola

The same bot-free capture, but transcription and summaries run on your computer. No account or subscription.

Otter or Fireflies

Nothing joins your call, the audio stays on your computer, and there are no minute caps.

Apple Notes

Notes records your microphone, so with headphones on it misses the other side of the call. LokalBot records both sides and keeps every meeting searchable.

Rewind or Screenpipe

Rewind's Mac app is discontinued. LokalBot starts with app and window activity; screen text and screenshots are opt-in and deleted after 14 days by default.

Hyprnote, Meetily, or OpenWhispr

Also open source. Here everything runs on your computer by default, and speaker separation, meeting detection, and a local MCP server are free.

Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, or MacWhisper

Dictation runs on your computer and comes with meeting notes and recall, for free. For transcribing files you already have, MacWhisper is the better tool.

System requirements · Setup help and feedback

A few questions.

What does it capture from my screen?

New installs start with activity-only tracking. You can turn tracking off, save visible text without pictures, or add encrypted screenshots. Text and screenshots are opt-in. Excluded apps and sites, password managers, and focused password fields are skipped. Private browser windows are captured like any other window, so exclude the browser or site to keep them out. Captured text and screenshots are deleted after 14 days by default. Moments you explicitly save stay until you unsave or delete them.

Does anything leave my computer?

Recordings and built-in processing stay local. Network access covers model downloads, update checks, and optional agent setup. A remote model receives the context needed for a request only after you approve its server. Approved agent commands can also access the network. Connected CLI or MCP tools have their own data handling; meeting and screen-memory access have separate permissions. See the privacy policy.

Can I choose my own models?

Yes. Start with the built-in local models, choose another downloaded model, or connect Ollama, an OpenAI-compatible server, or, on Mac, Apple Intelligence. Summaries and chat can also run on your own ChatGPT or Claude plan, with no API key. A remote server or plan gets context only after you approve it. Transcription, summaries, and autocomplete have their own model choices. See the transcription model guide and offline setup guide.

Which languages does it support?

Transcription covers 52 languages and dialects with the default Qwen3-ASR model, and alternatives such as SenseVoice for Chinese, Japanese, and Korean are available in Settings. Meeting notes are written in the meeting's language unless you choose another, and the app itself is translated into 24 languages. On Mac, autocomplete also keeps up in German, French, Italian, Spanish, and more.

Does it work in mainland China?

Yes. It records calls in Tencent Meeting, VooV Meeting, Feishu, Lark, DingTalk, and WeCom, keyword search finds Chinese, Japanese, and Korean words anywhere in a phrase, and models can download from HF-Mirror or ModelScope instead of Hugging Face. Choose the source in Settings → Models.

Is LokalBot free?

Yes. LokalBot is free and open source under GPLv3. There is no account, subscription, or LokalBot server. See the fact sheet for what it includes.

What does it run on?

Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) with macOS 15 or later, plus Windows 10 and 11 (x64) and 64-bit Linux in alpha. Intel Macs are not supported, and a few features are Mac only for now. Check the system requirements for memory, disk space, and what each platform supports.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. LokalBot records your microphone and the meeting's audio on separate tracks; on Windows and Linux the second track is the meeting app where it can be separated, and otherwise everything your computer plays. Let participants know you're recording and get their consent. Read how recording works.
