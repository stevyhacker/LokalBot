Personal Context

It remembers how you work.

An encrypted list of your facts, preferences, goals, and principles, so Ask fits your work. Add to it yourself, or let the overnight review learn from your own words.

  • Only your own words. Never other speakers', and read by a local model unless you approve a server.
  • You see the source. Every learned item shows its line. Facts and goals from meetings save directly; the rest waits for your OK.
  • Yours to turn off. Learning is on by default, up to three items a night. One switch stops it.

A hand with the writing, too.

Finish the sentence

Optional autocomplete that draws on the message you're answering and, if you let it, your meetings. Tab takes the next word.

How autocomplete works

Talk instead of typing

Hold ⌥ Space (Alt+Space on Windows and Linux), talk, and release. Your words appear at the cursor, transcribed locally.

Rewrite what you selected

Select text in any app and press ⌃⇧ W (Ctrl+Alt+Space on Windows and Linux) to fix, shorten, or translate it, or draft a reply from what your meetings say.

Draft the follow-up

Turn a meeting into a draft you review and send, or keep notes in Obsidian or Logseq.

Agent access

Works with your agents.

Claude Code, Codex, or any MCP client can search your meetings through the bundled lokalbot-cli or its MCP server.

  • Read-only. Agents can search and read your library, not change it.
  • Off until you turn it on in Settings. Screen memory and the Personal Context you share each need their own permission.
  • Your agent, your provider. Results go to its model provider, so connect only clients you trust.

Open any action item in Claude Code or Codex, prompt ready to review. Optional Agent Mode works on your files with your approval.

# Connect Claude Code over MCP
$ claude mcp add lokalbot -- lokalbot-cli mcp

# Or search from any shell or agent
$ lokalbot-cli search "database decision"
$ lokalbot-cli actions --owner me

Privacy

Your recordings stay on your computer.

Recording, transcription, and the built-in models run on your computer. Screen text and screenshots are optional, and you choose what to capture and how long to keep it.

Models and updates need a download. A remote model gets context only after the app asks. Agents and external tools have their own permissions and data handling.

System requirements · Setup help and feedback

A few questions.

What does it capture from my screen?

New installs track only app and window activity, and you can turn that off. Visible text and encrypted screenshots are opt-in, and are deleted after 14 days by default unless you save the moment. Excluded apps and sites, password managers, and focused password fields are skipped. Private browser windows are captured like any other, so exclude the browser or site to keep them out.

Does anything leave my computer?

Recordings and built-in processing stay local. The network is used for model downloads, update checks, and optional agent setup. A remote model gets context only after you approve its server. Approved agent commands can also use the network, and connected CLI or MCP tools have their own data handling. See the privacy policy.

Can I choose my own models?

Yes. Use the built-in local models, another downloaded model, Ollama, an OpenAI-compatible server, or, on Mac, Apple Intelligence. Summaries and chat can also run on your own ChatGPT or Claude plan. A remote server or plan gets context only after you approve it. See the transcription model guide and offline setup guide.

Which languages does it support?

Transcription covers 52 languages and dialects with the default Qwen3-ASR model. Notes follow the meeting's language unless you choose another, and the app is translated into 24 languages. On Mac, autocomplete also writes in German, French, Italian, Spanish, and more.

Does it work in mainland China?

Yes. It records calls in Tencent Meeting, VooV Meeting, Feishu, Lark, DingTalk, and WeCom, search finds Chinese, Japanese, and Korean words, and models can download from HF-Mirror or ModelScope.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. LokalBot records your microphone and the meeting's audio on separate tracks; on Windows and Linux the second track is the meeting app where it can be separated, and otherwise everything your computer plays. Tell participants you're recording and get their consent. Read how recording works.
