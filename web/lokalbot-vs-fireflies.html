LokalBot vs Fireflies

Fireflies is built for teams: its notetaker joins your calls, transcribes in more than 100 languages, and pushes notes into CRMs and project tools. If your company runs on a shared call library, it's a strong choice. LokalBot is for the opposite need — notes that stay on your Mac. It captures the call from your Mac's audio with no bot, and runs transcription and summaries locally.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Fireflies if…

A team call library with a lot of plumbing.

  • You want every sales or support call logged to your CRM automatically
  • Your calls span 100+ languages
  • You need SSO, audit logs, and admin roles for a large team
  • You work across Windows, the web, and your phone

Pick LokalBot if…

For the calls you'd rather not upload.

  • Your calls involve clients, patients, or sources whose words can't go to a third-party cloud
  • No bot means no “who invited the notetaker?” moment
  • Free forever — no seats, no storage minutes
  • Dictation, a day timeline, and autocomplete come in the same app

Questions

The short answers.

Why not use Fireflies without the bot?

You can capture without the bot through Fireflies' Chrome extension, but the recording is still processed and stored in Fireflies' cloud under your account. LokalBot keeps the audio, transcript, and summary on your Mac.

Can LokalBot push notes into my CRM?

Not directly. LokalBot exports meetings as Markdown, can write a daily memory note to a folder, and exposes a read-only CLI and MCP server so local tools and agents can read your library. Anything beyond that is up to you.

Will people on the call know LokalBot is recording?

No — nothing appears in the call, which is why you need to tell participants yourself. On a fresh install, LokalBot asks through a notification before it records a detected meeting.
