# LokalBot Changelog

## [Ask LokalBot and Draft Reply] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Added Ask LokalBot, which answers a question from your meetings with LokalBot's local model and lists the meetings it used.
- Added Draft Reply, which drafts your reply to the selected message (or the clipboard) from what your meetings say, ready to paste.
- Both need a LokalBot release newer than 0.10.10 with the app running.
- When meeting access is off, the message points to the "Allow external agents to read your LokalBot data" switch, with Meetings ticked.

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Added Quick Recall for searching LokalBot meeting titles and summaries.
- Added Recent Meetings for browsing the latest captured meetings.
- Added actions to copy matching snippets and reveal meeting files in Finder.
