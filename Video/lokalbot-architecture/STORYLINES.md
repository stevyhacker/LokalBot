# Storylines

The brief (2026-11-10): "app architecture with all local llms 60s explainer video animated and
visual". Three storylines were offered and the answer was "Make all 3 variants", narrated in the
cloned voice. They are three films, not three cuts of one: each has its own world, device and form.
All three say the same facts (`FACTS.md`) and end on the same card.

## A. One Sentence (`one-sentence/`)

The worked example. Someone on a call says "The offsite is in Lisbon, May 12 to 14." and the film
follows that sentence through everything LokalBot does to it: two recorded tracks, speaker turns,
words with their times, the notes, a vector in the database, the end of a sentence typed days
later, an agent's answer. The viewer learns the pipeline by watching one thing change shape.

- First two seconds: a call window, one tile speaking, the sentence arriving as its caption in amber.
- Beats: call → two tracks → who spoke → words → notes → meaning and the database → typing → the
  agent's gate → pull back: the whole route inside one Mac → end card.
- Form: one continuous take. The world is a 3 × 3 map inside the Mac and the camera travels it in a
  spiral, so the pull-back shows the route it took.
- Look: inside the Mac is brand slate; the sentence is the only amber thing, in every form it takes;
  the pull-back reveals the Mac standing on mint.
- Sound: the voice, key ticks where text is typed, one whoosh on the pull-back.

## B. Inside the Mac (`inside-the-mac/`)

The object is the explanation. People ask which cloud their meetings go to; the film opens the Mac
instead. Under the app there are three layers: the model files, drawn to scale by size, the engines
they plug into, and the library folder. Then the edge of the Mac: what comes in, and the three doors
that stay shut until the person opens them.

- First two seconds: an isometric MacBook with the real app on its screen, a dashed cloud above it
  and the question "Where do your meetings go?".
- Beats: the cloud struck out → the Mac opens into layers → five model files seat in a row, to scale
  → 9.6 GB → three engines under them → the library's folder tree → the edge and its doors → it
  closes → end card.
- Form: one frame that rebuilds: an exploded isometric view, with each layer turning to face the
  camera while it is explained.
- Look: a light ground, the Mac in slate, model files in the robot's mint; amber is "your work".
- Sound: the voice, a soft landing for each model file, one whoosh when the Mac opens.

## C. Taking Turns (`taking-turns/`) (the bold one)

The part nobody draws: how five models share one laptop's memory. The models are files on a shelf
(the disk); memory is a desk above it; a clock runs through a working day. During the call nothing
is loaded. After it the speech models come up, work, and leave two minutes later; the language model
writes the notes and waits ten minutes; the autocomplete model stays while there is room; a broker
keeps the desk under half of memory and lets what the person is waiting for go first.

- First two seconds: five model files stacked in a tower beside a small laptop, and the question.
- Beats: the tower → it lies down on the shelf, 9.6 GB → the call (only recording) → speech models up
  and gone in two minutes → the language model, ten minutes → autocomplete stays → the broker's
  budget and queue → the day at rest → end card.
- Form: one stage held for the whole film (shelf, desk, clock), with close-ups; every model makes
  the same move, so the pattern is the point.
- Look: deep teal ground, model files in mint, amber only on the model that is working right now.
- Sound: the voice, a soft landing when a model reaches memory, key ticks for the typing.
