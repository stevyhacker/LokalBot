LokalBot records two tracks: your microphone, and the meeting app's audio. No bot joins the call.
Nemotron separates the voices, up to eight speakers.
Qwen3-ASR transcribes every turn, with its timestamp.
Then Qwen3.5, on the built-in llama.cpp server, writes the decisions, action items, and open questions.
Or swap in Apple Intelligence, Ollama, or a server you approve.
It all lands in a local SQLite library, indexed by keyword, and by meaning with Harrier embeddings.
Screen text is opt-in. It's read from accessibility, with on-device OCR as a fallback.
Credentials are redacted, screenshots are encrypted, and both expire after fourteen days by default.
Search blends keyword and meaning, and every result opens the exact moment.
Agents get read-only access through a CLI and MCP server, off until you turn it on.
No account, no telemetry, and remote servers only with your approval.
LokalBot. Free and open source.
