## What’s new

- **More reliable meeting audio capture.** LokalBot now finds the real audio-producing helper processes used by apps such as Microsoft Teams, attaches system-audio capture on manual starts, retries deterministic targets when audio processes change, and clears stale silent-audio warnings once sound arrives. Sustained-audio confirmation also avoids creating empty recordings when a communications app merely launches or keeps an idle stream open.
- **Cleaner speaker-separated transcripts.** Conservative speaker-bleed filtering removes duplicated remote speech picked up by the microphone while preserving the user’s real reply. Mixed echo-and-speech segments are trimmed instead of discarded, with character-aware matching for Chinese and Japanese.
- **Grounded action items and decisions.** Outcome extraction now covers the complete transcript with token-aware chunks, resumable checkpoints, evidence validation, and deduplication. Summary sections stay synchronized with the cited outcome cards, tentative points remain open questions, and user-owned prose uses natural first-person wording such as “I will” instead of “Me will.”
- **Find anything on a meeting page.** The visible search control and Command-F now search and highlight the meeting title, metadata, status, action items, decisions, notes, summary details, transcript engine, timestamps, speaker names, and transcript text, with Previous and Next navigation across every match.
- **Calendar-assisted speaker matching.** With Calendar access enabled, attendee names and emails can disambiguate speaker suggestions. Unambiguous one-to-one matches are applied automatically, ambiguous speakers remain user-selectable, and attendee emails stay in local meeting metadata rather than being sent to inference providers.
- **Day Digests that stay current.** Manual, scheduled, and command-line generation now share one lifecycle and freshness model. New meeting summaries or outcomes can trigger a quiet repair of an outdated digest, while task cards show “Show more” only when the rendered text truly overflows.
- **Clearer model readiness and stronger inference recovery.** Transcription, Think, and Autocomplete roles now report consistent download, preparation, ready, and error states across onboarding and Settings. Built-in and approved remote inference share the same preparation and privacy path, and a failed current Dreaming brief now offers a bounded recovery action.
- **Agent sessions you can safely resume.** Agent Mode adds session-only approval levels, per-tool allowances, searchable saved-session history, restoration in the original workspace, and actionable guidance when a model generation test fails.
- **Private OpenRouter routing by default.** New and migrated installations send `data_collection: deny` by default. Following the OpenRouter account policy is an explicit, confirmed opt-in, and failure guidance reflects the active privacy mode.
- **More dependable navigation and text input.** Atomic handoffs keep Ask queries, meeting citation seeks, retained moments, and Agent launch context attached to the correct destination. The command palette no longer double-enters typed characters.
- **More ways to connect LokalBot.** The repository now includes a Claude Code `/recall` plugin, a Homebrew cask, a Raycast extension for Quick Recall and Recent Meetings, and Hugging Face publishing and benchmark assets, alongside refreshed documentation, screenshots, and product video.
- **Updated local AI and app runtimes.** FluidAudio, WhisperKit, Qwen3Speech, MLX Swift, sherpa-onnx/ONNX Runtime, Sparkle, Bun, the Pi agent runtime, SwiftNIO Extras, and release automation dependencies were refreshed and repinned.
