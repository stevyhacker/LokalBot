## What’s new

- **Meeting briefs from Gmail (Mac).** With a Gmail account connected in Settings → Personal Context → Email, the Today card for your next meeting shows your recent conversations with its attendees, matched by email address, and flags the ones waiting on your reply. Click a conversation to open it in Gmail. The brief is written only by a model running on your Mac, from short excerpts with signatures, quoted replies and card numbers removed. Google is still reviewing LokalBot’s Gmail access, so sign-in shows an unverified-app warning and is limited to the first 100 users. [How it works](https://www.lokalbot.com/explainers/gmail-meeting-briefs.pdf)

## Fixes

- **No Documents prompt at launch (Mac).** LokalBot no longer asks for access to your Documents folder when it starts. A Whisper model cache that versions before September kept there moves to Application Support the next time Whisper is prepared.
- **OpenRouter reasoning levels (Mac).** The Think reasoning menu shows Max again for OpenRouter models that offer it, and a request set to Max is no longer lowered to High.
- **Security fixes (Windows and Linux).** Trusted certificate roots no longer include the compromised emSign certificates. Agent Mode rejects a server address that only looked local because of a backslash or a user name in it. A malformed Retry-After header can no longer crash the app. On Linux, your library is readable only by your user account.
- **Reprocessing keeps your choices (Windows and Linux).** Transcribing a meeting again keeps its speaker names, “My microphone is me” applies everywhere, and action items from the replaced transcript are set aside. Queued summaries follow the automatic-summary setting after a restart, and processing works in libraries created by older Mac versions.
- **Action owners in other languages (Windows and Linux).** Choosing You as an action’s owner in a non-English interface keeps the action yours.
- **Speaker wording (Windows and Linux).** Speaker labels in most languages now mean a person speaking, not a loudspeaker.

Windows and Linux remain in alpha. The Windows app is not yet code-signed.
