## What’s new

- **Clearer Quick Recall.** Refined result rows, app icons, timestamps, and keyboard hints make saved screen context and meeting results easier to scan. Matching saved moments appear first, open the matching capture, and no longer repeat the same source group.
- **More reliable speaker evidence.** Fixed Google Meet window matching when capture and Accessibility report different titles. Restored waveform checks for microphone speech that could otherwise be mistaken for a separate speaker, while preserving explicitly confirmed identities.
- **Easier speaker naming.** A new **Name speakers** action in the meeting overview lets you play a voice and choose a calendar guest. Name-like email addresses offer editable suggestions labeled **From email**; names are applied only after you select and save them.
- **Clearer capture diagnostics.** Meetings now explain when visual speaker identification was attempted but captured no usable observations, with a direct route to review speakers.
- **Updated website and guides.** Refreshed product examples and screenshots show how meeting transcripts and saved screen context work together as searchable workday memory.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/stevyhacker/lokalbot/compare/v0.8.1...v0.8.2
