## What’s new

- **A calmer Windows and Linux app.** The Windows and Linux app gets the Mac's redesign from 0.13.0. Actions has its own page in the sidebar, with your open count. Today puts what needs you, today's meetings, the day so far and yesterday's brief in separate panels. A meeting opens with its player on top, then decisions and actions split into yours and other people's; you name voices with **Name speakers…** instead of a Review tab, and exports are under Share. Timeline shows the day's page beside a sessions list you can widen. People leads with your next meeting and what you and each person owe each other, and Projects with where each one stands. Ask has one field where Enter always asks. Settings opens in its own window, with one Privacy & permissions page, and setup takes three steps and downloads nothing until you ask. Ctrl+K opens Quick Recall, which replaces the command palette and answers in its own window with Ctrl+Enter, and the tray menu shows what's recording, a meeting coming up and your two latest meetings. [How it works](https://www.lokalbot.com/explainers/windows-linux-app-tour.pdf)

## Improvements

- **Local models on llama.cpp 1.0 (all computers).** On a Windows PC with a Radeon RX 6600 XT (Vulkan), Qwen3.5 0.8B writes about 1.8× faster and reads prompts about 2.3× faster. On the Mac, answers and speed are unchanged. On Linux and Windows without a GPU a few words of an answer can differ, and search indexes stay valid.
- **Qwen transcription uses less memory (Mac).** After each 25-second window, Qwen3-ASR 1.7B used to keep about 2.9 GB of spare memory; now it keeps 4 MB, and each window is about 13% faster. Transcripts and word timings are identical.
- **Parakeet keeps the start of long recordings (Mac).** It no longer drops speech at the very start of a long recording, it repairs short gaps where the pieces of a recording meet, and an interrupted model download resumes instead of starting over.
- **Agent Mode on pi 1.5.2 (all computers).** Large tool calls and file reads use less CPU and memory. Approvals and what Agent Mode may read are unchanged.
- **Fixed on Windows and Linux.** Pause and Resume inside the app now update the paused state, right-to-left languages keep the numbers in durations and meeting lines, and Back no longer hides under the tab switcher in windows narrower than about 1,100 pixels.

## When you update

- **Windows and Linux layout.** Settings is a separate window, and a meeting has no Review tab: name voices with **Name speakers…**, and nothing changes until you press **Save names**. The command palette is gone: Ctrl+K opens Quick Recall, where Enter opens a match and Ctrl+Enter answers. In Ask, Enter asks the model even about a single word; press ↓ and then Enter to open a match. A full question with All sources and Any time also reads your work memory and About you, as on the Mac. Plan in Agent moved from Today to Actions.
- **Agent Mode (Windows and Linux).** When the task's model runs on this computer, read-only lookups of your own library (one `lokalbot-cli brief`, `list`, `get` or `search` command) now run without asking, at every access level. With a server or subscription model they still ask, and so does every lookup on Windows without Git Bash.
- **People (Windows and Linux)** keeps the next seven days of calendar events in memory, while calendar use is on, to show who is coming up. Nothing is stored, and personal appointments are never read.
- **Tray menu (Windows and Linux).** A meeting coming up is shown without attendee names, because other apps on a Linux desktop can read tray menu text.
- **Agent Mode runtime (all computers)** sets itself up again once: about 65 MB on the Mac, 75 MB on Linux and 80 MB on Windows.
- **llama.cpp (Windows and Linux)** downloads its new runtime in the background after you update. With an NVIDIA GPU, set CUDA up again when asked. The newer CUDA build now needs a CUDA 13.5 driver for Turing cards, A100 and H100; with an older driver they get the CUDA 12 build.

The redesigned Windows and Linux screens were compared with the Mac's on Linux under a virtual X11 display, with the demo library, in light and dark and in Arabic. On one Windows PC the release build processed a meeting on Vulkan and showed 20 pages correctly, but nobody clicked through it by hand. Not checked on any machine: real Wayland, KDE and GNOME desktops, the Windows tray and setup's access checks, model downloads from the new setup, a real calendar and audio playback. Strings only the Windows and Linux app uses were translated by machine and have had no native review. Follow-through, Hand off, Live Radar and Gmail in briefs remain Mac-only. The CUDA builds were checked by hash and contents only, not on an NVIDIA GPU, and speaker separation wasn't re-run on real recordings after its update.

Windows and Linux remain in alpha, and the Windows app is not yet code-signed. The Android app is on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.dotenv.lokalbot.companion) and here as a direct download for Android 10 or later; the two are signed with different keys, so one can't update the other. The iPhone and iPad app isn't in the App Store yet.
