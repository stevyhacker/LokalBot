## What’s new

- **Close the window and keep working.** Closing LokalBot’s main window now leaves the app running, so background capture can continue. Reopen it from the menu bar or Dock; use Quit or ⌘Q when you want to stop the app.
- **See how much autocomplete helps.** Settings → Writing now shows accepted words, how often you use suggestions, their share of your typing, and estimated time saved. Charts show trends over time, with breakdowns by app category and model. Statistics start with this update and stay on your Mac as counts, without saving typed text, app names, sites, or window titles.
- **MCP connections work with Claude Code and Claude Desktop.** Fixed a connection timeout that prevented these clients from getting a response from `lokalbot-cli mcp`.
- **Clearer Writing and Advanced settings.** The autocomplete switch sits beside its model and permission status. Context, writing profile, health checks, and resource usage are easier to scan.
- **Less clutter when completing actions.** The persistent Undo bar has been removed. To reopen a completed action, go to Actions and choose All statuses.

The website also has clearer setup and switching guidance, new guides for using meeting notes with coding agents and Obsidian, and better mobile navigation.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/stevyhacker/lokalbot/compare/v0.10.7...v0.10.8
