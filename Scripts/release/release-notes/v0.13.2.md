## What’s new

- **Import meetings from other apps (Mac).** **Recording → Import Meetings From** brings notes, transcripts with named speakers, action items and recordings from Granola, Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, Screenpipe, Zoom, Voice Memos and MacWhisper into your library. LokalBot only reads the files, nothing goes online, and importing again adds nothing twice. Agents can use `lokalbot-cli import`, which asks you before anything is written. [How it works](https://www.lokalbot.com/explainers/meeting-import.pdf)

## Improvements

- **Transcription without MLX (Mac).** Qwen3-ASR runs on the bundled llama.cpp. 1.7B keeps its accuracy and uses about 2.3 GB of memory instead of 7.6–8.5 GB. 0.6B is more accurate. Word timing for speakers uses a CoreML aligner with about a quarter of the memory, and the app is 15% smaller.
- **Yandex Telemost and MTS Link calls are detected** on Mac and Windows. Thanks to region23 for both.
- **A recording stops after 15 minutes of silence (Mac),** even if call detection stalls. Very long recordings no longer hang Stop or the Meetings screen.
- **Timeline and day digests read Codex's compressed session logs,** so older Codex work shows up on every platform.
- **Fixed:** the Projects list keeps its width when you select a project (Mac).

## When you update

- **Speech models download again once (Mac):** 2.52 GB for Qwen3-ASR 1.7B, 1.02 GB for 0.6B and 1.08 GB for the word aligner. The old copies are deleted after the new ones verify. Qwen3-ASR transcribes about 10–30% slower than before.
- **Granite Speech 5 Turbo is gone (Mac).** If you used it, LokalBot switches to Parakeet v2 and deletes its 0.95 GB folder.

Importing and Telemost/MTS Link detection haven't been tried on real exports and live calls yet, and every import source is partial because none of these apps documents its export format. Windows and Linux remain in alpha, and the Windows app isn't code-signed. The Android app is on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.dotenv.lokalbot.companion) and here for Android 10 or later, signed with different keys, so one can't update the other. The iPhone and iPad app isn't in the App Store yet.
