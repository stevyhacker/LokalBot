## What’s new

- **Manual Teams recordings capture the call audio again.** LokalBot can reach the audio stream used by the new Teams app when you start a recording by hand. It keeps that stream steady through short bursts from Teams helpers and can still switch to another verified meeting source.
- **Action items find their owner more reliably.** Commitments are matched to the speaker’s own words across transcript rows, including interrupted and run-on speech. Questions, reported speech and delegated tasks retain their separate ownership checks. A task repeated in its source is merged into the task already extracted from it.
- **Cleaner transcripts during quiet moments.** Placeholder speaker labels stay out of transcription vocabulary, and rows that only echo the vocabulary hint are filtered before they can enter meeting notes or speaker review.
- **Day digests are easier to scan.** Tasks are grouped by status, next steps sit with their task, blockers stand out, and summaries open on demand. Existing digests get the new layout without regeneration.
- **Chrome autocomplete follows the line you’re typing on.** In fields that report no caret position, on-device text recognition can locate the line when Screen Recording is already granted. Suggestion placement and background sampling also handle wrapped text, display edges and scaling more accurately.
- **Meeting navigation is clearer.** Selected meeting subtitles remain readable, and the Summary, Transcript, Notes and Review tabs have equal widths and a consistent selection indicator.
- **Custom models explain when they need re-adding.** Saved model entries with an unusable download source are marked “Needs re-adding” and explain how to replace them, instead of offering a download or retry that cannot succeed.
- **Updated speech, local inference and Agent Mode runtimes.** Qwen transcription responds to cancellation, Nemotron uses the updated model export and runtime fixes, and the bundled local inference and Agent Mode dependencies are refreshed. Agent Mode installs its updated runtime through the normal explicit setup.
