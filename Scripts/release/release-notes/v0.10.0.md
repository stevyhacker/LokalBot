## What’s new

- **Meeting notes adapt to the model you use.** Notes are now split to fit the selected model’s real context window instead of one small size for every model. The window comes from OpenRouter’s live model data, from servers that report it (vLLM, Groq, Together, Mistral, LM Studio and llama-server on your Mac), or from Cerebras’s and OpenAI’s documentation, and each request stays within the provider’s structured-output limits. A 20-minute meeting now takes one or two requests on hosted models such as Qwen 3.8 27B, GPT-5.6 Luna and GLM 5.3 Flash, instead of four.
- **Stuck summaries recover.** A summary part that ran out of output before writing anything is read again from the start instead of being asked to continue from nothing, and **Summarize again** retries a part that an earlier run had stopped. On Automatic reasoning, notes no longer spend their whole answer on hidden reasoning with models that think by default.
- **Choose a reasoning level per model.** Settings → Models and the Agent composer offer the reasoning levels the selected model accepts, from Off to Max. Automatic keeps each task’s own setting.
- **Day digests stay put.** Re-summarizing a meeting, editing its transcript, ticking an action item, saving a moment or new coding-agent activity no longer deletes that day’s digest. It stays visible and is marked out of date until it refreshes. Digests also outlast the screen-text retention window, like overnight reviews. Deleting a meeting, a capture or saved agent sessions still removes the digest built from them.
- **Fewer wrong autocomplete suggestions.** A suggestion is held back when the model is unsure of its first word. New installs use the visible text above the field for better suggestions, and private windows are never read. Autocomplete stays quiet in open and save dialogs, clears a suggestion left alone for a minute, and points out when macOS’s own inline predictions are on.
- **Open a meeting in Claude or Codex.** The meeting’s More menu starts a Claude or Codex conversation with the meeting’s title, summary and transcript already typed in; nothing is sent until you press Send. It can also copy the meeting folder’s path, and workspace menus use the standard menu font.
- **Agent Mode asks before every tool** except reading files in your workspace, including tools from MCP servers, unless you choose Full access.
- **The main window resizes properly on macOS 26.** Sections with a split view can shrink to their real minimum width again.
- **Updated on-device runtimes.** MLX Swift 0.32.3 and MLX-LM 3.32.3 for on-device speech, plus updated networking and model-loading dependencies.
