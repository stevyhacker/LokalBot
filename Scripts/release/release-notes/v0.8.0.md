## What’s new

- **Redesigned workspaces.** A clearer Today view, reorganized meeting and timeline panes, searchable settings, and more consistent navigation make it easier to move from your daily brief to the source material and next action. Meeting notes, transcript search, playback, and export stay close at hand.
- **Actions that connect across meetings.** Related commitments now form action threads while retaining each source meeting. Review, filter, correct, and complete actions with Undo; changes spanning multiple meetings require confirmation, and failed saves remain visible and retryable.
- **More deliberate Search and Ask.** Choose Words or Meaning search, open source evidence without starting playback or a model request, and explicitly ask about the selected context. Follow-ups and retries preserve the displayed scope, and completed actions remain available in Ask.
- **Daily memory that stays in sync.** Digest, briefs, exports, and routines share daily evidence covering meetings, activity, saved moments, and action status. Corrections and deletions refresh generated material while preserving manually edited files. Today’s briefs also have cleaner text presentation.
- **Restored meeting waveforms and steadier live transcripts.** Recorded meetings show audio waveforms again. Live transcription handles continuous speech and queued chunks more reliably, preserves progress across pause and retry, and shows clearer recording and transcription status.
- **Clearer setup and controls.** Streamlined setup preserves existing preferences and approvals. Model labels reflect the active operation, Agent approvals explain their effects, and retention changes show affected data before deletion while preserving saved moments by default. Keyboard navigation, accessibility, narrow layouts, and dictation rehearsal have also been improved.
