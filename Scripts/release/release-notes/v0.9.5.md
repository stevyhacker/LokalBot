## What’s new

- **Autocomplete keeps up with your typing.** Suggestions follow the line and font you’re typing in, including TextEdit and Telegram. The model prepares when you focus a field, and the next words stay ready as you accept a suggestion.
- **Writing can use the context you choose.** Autocomplete and dictation’s Compose mode can draw on nearby visible text, relevant meeting notes, and saved work memory. Each source has its own opt-in. Unrelated facts stay out of suggestions, and Compose uses context for writing instructions without adding claims to ordinary dictated sentences. Gemma 4 E2B Base is the new recommended autocomplete model; the lighter LFM2.5 option remains available.
- **See what was on screen during a meeting.** The meeting’s screen list shows readable page titles, thumbnails, and estimated time on screen. Jump to that point in the recording, open the retained capture, or reopen a page whose address was saved. Chrome page addresses are captured more reliably.
- **Recording recovers from more interruptions.** LokalBot keeps trying to reconnect a microphone that drops out. Rejoining a call after it ends, or moving to another Google Meet room, can start the next recording correctly.
- **Meeting notes keep more of what matters.** All extracted action items are retained, and a long meeting’s first takeaways span the whole recording. Correcting an action preserves the summary. Filler phrases no longer leave notes marked partial, and due dates don’t invent a year that nobody said. Very short speaker regions rejected by the transcription engine no longer fail the whole meeting.
- **Ask and connected tools can reach more of your library.** Ask can read whole meetings, meeting filters show every match, and CLI and MCP searches support keyword search. MCP pages long transcripts and reports when timeline results are shortened. Date-based queries follow your local day.
- **Day memory keeps separate work and catches up.** Digests retain multiple subjects, decisions, and commitments within busy stretches of the day. Missed daily note exports catch up, and finalized digests can be added to past days. Overnight reviews and saved work memory survive screen-text expiration while still respecting source deletion and corrections.
- **More language coverage and stronger redaction.** Serbian is available as a transcription language, and screen text recognizes Chinese and Japanese. Screen capture excludes password managers by bundle ID and redacts more credential formats, valid payment-card numbers, and IBANs. Agent Mode shell commands no longer inherit the model API key.
