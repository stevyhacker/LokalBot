## What’s new

- **A rebuilt Ask workspace.** Ask and Search now share one focused surface with exact or semantic search, source and date controls, persistent question history, clearer citations, and a calmer editorial conversation layout. Answers support continuous text selection, and long conversations are easier to navigate.
- **More dependable meeting recording.** Bluetooth headsets now use encoder-safe recording settings, Teams autostart tolerates natural conversational pauses, and live meetings keep and recover the correct system-audio stream instead of ending or bouncing between silent helper processes.
- **Optional microphone echo cancellation.** LokalBot can subtract speaker playback from the microphone track before transcription, reducing duplicated remote speech. It stays off by default and safely falls back to the original track when cancellation does not measurably improve the audio.
- **Better transcription control.** A saved transcription-context field now reaches Whisper and Qwen3-ASR, model data lives under Application Support, and queued, retried, and resumed jobs preserve the user’s explicit Transcribe or Transcribe & Summarize choice.
