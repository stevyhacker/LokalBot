## What’s new

- **A native Mac design, with your own theme and text size.** Meetings, Ask, Actions, Today, Agent, and Settings were refreshed with native controls in light and dark appearances. Settings adds theme and text-size preferences.
- **People and Projects.** New sidebar sections join calendar attendees, speaker names you applied, and action owners into one person, with what you owe each other, shared decisions, and shared meetings. Projects connect overnight Dream projects and recurring topics to their meetings, actions, and people.
- **Your work memory connects.** Search covers meeting notes and corrected outcomes. Spoken due phrases such as “Friday” resolve to dates, and overdue work comes first. Meetings show what was on screen during the call, and transcription recognizes names from your calendar and earlier meetings.
- **Day digests and Dreaming run on schedule with your approved model server.** Approving a remote model server once now covers scheduled digests and Dreaming. Missed days from the past week catch up, Dreaming catches up at launch while on power, and a card in the sidebar shows meeting processing, digests, Dreaming, downloads, and reindexing wherever you are in the app.
- **Better day digests.** Lighter but identifiable work is listed after the main tasks instead of disappearing, and work done with an AI assistant or coding agent counts as your own. Every work session is shown. Reasoning models get more room to answer, and a temporary provider error is retried instead of ending the digest early.
- **A simpler Timeline.** Rewind and the moments list are gone. The full digest shows directly, work sessions sit in a resizable rail on the right, and deleting a time range moved to Raw capture.
- **Browser and Chrome activity is recorded again.** Browser, web-app, and private windows keep their app names and titles instead of showing as Private, and Chrome screen memory is captured reliably again. App and site exclusions, secure fields, and credential redaction still apply.
- **Library repairs.** Meetings hidden from search after an interrupted delete are found again. Recordings that were never transcribed, or were only partly transcribed after a merge, are queued automatically. Only one copy of LokalBot can open your library at a time, and development builds use their own library.
- **More reliable meeting notes.** Action items you commit to are assigned to you from the actual obligation, not an earlier status line. Authorized meeting recordings are no longer lost silently when an audio writer stalls.
- **Readable supporting text.** Speaker excerpts, meeting details, and sidebar status text meet 4.5:1 contrast in both appearances.
- **Updated components.** The bundled llama.cpp runtime is updated to v0.5.0, and Qwen3-ASR 1.7B and Parakeet v2 show their correct download sizes.
