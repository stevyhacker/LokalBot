## What’s new

- **A calmer Mac app.** Every screen was redrawn to show each thing once and lead with what you came for. Actions has its own page in the sidebar, with your open count. Today puts what needs you, today's meetings, the day so far and yesterday's brief in separate panels. A meeting opens on one header with its player, then decisions and actions split into yours and other people's; you name voices with **Name speaker…** instead of a Review tab, and exports are under Share. Settings opens in its own window, with one Privacy & permissions page. Ask has one field where Return always asks, ⌘K opens Quick Recall, and on Timeline you can drag the divider to widen the sessions list. [How it works](https://www.lokalbot.com/explainers/mac-app-tour.pdf)
- **Search your coding agents' notes (Mac, Windows and Linux).** Claude Code and Codex keep notes for their own later sessions. LokalBot can now read them, so Ask, Quick Recall and other agents (`lokalbot-cli search-notes`, the MCP tool `search_agent_notes`) find what one agent already wrote down. Instruction files such as `CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md` are never read, detected credentials are redacted, and the notes never enter the day digest or Personal Context. Off by default: turn on **Read coding agent notes** in Settings → Screen & activity on the Mac, or Settings → Day Memory on Windows and Linux. Other agents need their own switch. [How it works](https://www.lokalbot.com/explainers/agent-notes.pdf)

## Improvements

- **Serbian and Croatian calls stay in their language (Mac).** On auto-detect, Qwen wrote parts of a Serbian and Croatian call in Chinese, Japanese or Cyrillic, and Whisper filled pauses with "Thank you." Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian and Montenegrin now count as one language and are written in Latin script, short pieces of a track keep the language the whole track settled on, Whisper's English stock phrases are dropped from tracks in other languages, and pauses without speech are skipped. On that call, rows in Chinese, Japanese or Hindi script went from 46 to 1, and rows with Cyrillic from 36 to none.
- **Autocomplete stays out of verification code fields (Mac, Windows and Linux).** It now also reads a field's placeholder, label and id, so a field such as "Enter code", OTP or 2FA gets no suggestion, and nothing is suggested while the text before the caret is only digits (a code, a phone number, an amount).
- **Setup downloads on its own (Windows and Linux).** The welcome window starts downloading the models your choices need as soon as it opens, so you can start while they finish, and a failed download offers Retry. Autocomplete now defaults to Gemma 4 E2B, as on the Mac, instead of LFM2.5 1.2B.
- **Updates install reliably (Windows and Linux).** An update started in the first minute after launch could lose its downloaded file and fail with "could not replace".
- **New typeface (Windows and Linux).** The interface uses Geist, and code, paths and logs use Geist Mono. A Hausa interface keeps Inter, which draws its hooked letters.

## When you update

- **Mac app layout.** Settings is a separate window (⌘, as before), and a meeting has no Review tab. In Ask, Return now asks the model about a single word too, where it used to open the first result; press ↓ and then Return to open a match. A full question reads everything in the chosen sources and dates, not only the matches listed under it.
- **Agent Mode (Mac).** When the task's model runs on this Mac, read-only lookups of your own library (one `lokalbot-cli brief`, `list`, `get` or `search` command) now run without asking, at every access level. With a server or subscription model they still ask.
- **People (Mac)** reads the next seven days of calendar events, while calendar use is on, to show who is coming up. Nothing is stored.
- **Setup and models (Mac).** An unfinished setup reopens at each launch until you finish or skip it. After a model you downloaded finishes, LokalBot loads it once and runs it on a built-in sample, unless a meeting is recording.
- **Meetings being transcribed (Mac).** A meeting still in processing when you update transcribes its tracks again.
- **Autocomplete model (Windows and Linux).** If you never turned autocomplete on, its model moves from LFM2.5 1.2B (0.73 GB) to Gemma 4 E2B (3.8 GB, for 16 GB of memory or more, and slower without a GPU). It downloads only when you turn autocomplete on. A downloaded LFM2.5 file stays, listed as unused in Model Storage; choose LFM2.5 again in Settings → Models to keep it.
- **Setup (Windows and Linux)** contacts Hugging Face, or the mirror you chose, as soon as its window opens.
- **Autocomplete after a number.** A message that starts with only a number gets no suggestion until you type a letter: "5" gets none, "5 m" does.

The redesigned Mac app passes the full hosted UI test suite, but its 692 new strings in 24 languages are machine translations no native speaker has reviewed, and the menu bar panel and People are covered only by unit tests and rendered screens. On Windows and Linux, Agent notes ran on test folders, not on real `~/.claude` or `~/.codex` folders, and its screens haven't been opened. The Serbian and Croatian changes were replayed on one real call but not checked in the installed app, and the code-field rule hasn't been tried on a real SMS-code page.

Windows and Linux remain in alpha, and the Windows app is not yet code-signed. Nothing in this release was run on Windows by hand. The Android app is on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.dotenv.lokalbot.companion) and here as a direct download for Android 10 or later; the two are signed with different keys, so one can't update the other. The iPhone and iPad app isn't in the App Store yet.
