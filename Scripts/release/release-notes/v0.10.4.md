## What’s new

- **Better autocomplete in English and Serbian Latin.** The hybrid decoder is now the standard whenever autocomplete is enabled, with improved partial-word completion and no extra experimental toggle. Gemma E2B remains the default local model.
- **Less waiting for suggestions.** The optimized hybrid reduced mean native generation time from **67.0 to 51.6 ms** and p95 from **142.4 to 98.9 ms** in the same-model, same-prompt benchmark. All 1,496 paired non-cancelled outputs matched. These are M4 Max measurements against the earlier hybrid; they exclude model loading, debounce, accessibility reads and overlay presentation. [Benchmark and limits](https://github.com/stevyhacker/lokalbot/blob/v0.10.4/Benchmarks/Cotyping/results/2026-10-11-latency/REPORT.md).
- **More reliable suggestion placement.** Improved on-screen caret detection, text-size matching and positioning keep ghost text aligned with what you type, including fixes tested in Chrome and Viber.
- **Optional Fun-ASR-Nano transcription.** Select a local Fun-ASR-Nano-2512 sherpa-onnx model folder in Models → Transcribe. LokalBot validates the folder and reads its weights in place; existing transcription choices are preserved.
