## What’s new

- **Claude meeting notes and day digests work through Anthropic’s native API.** Connections to `api.anthropic.com` now use the Messages API, fixing structured-output errors that prevented notes and digests from completing. Repeated requests over the same meeting or conversation can reuse Anthropic’s prompt cache, and Ask responses stream as they arrive.
- **Choose English or Simplified Chinese for the interface.** Settings → General → Appearance and first-run setup offer Follow System, English and 简体中文. The selection updates windows, app-owned menus and settings search immediately, while transcription language, generated notes and saved content keep their own settings.
- **A smaller on-device model for search by meaning.** EmbeddingGemma 2 replaces Harrier, with a smaller download and lower idle memory use. It improved Serbian and Montenegrin meeting queries in our evaluation, including queries over English meetings. The bundled llama.cpp runtime is updated to support it.
- **Find a speaker’s name as you type.** Suggested names can be searched by name or email, regardless of word order, case or accents, including common spellings of Serbian names.
- **Day digest controls are easier to use.** Ask About This Day, Write Digest and the digest actions menu use the regular control and text sizes.

### After updating

Search by meaning downloads the new model (about 310 MB) and rebuilds existing search vectors on your Mac. Keyword search remains available during the rebuild. After the new model has been verified, obsolete embedding-model files are removed to reclaim space.
