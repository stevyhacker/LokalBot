## What’s new

- **Choose your Ask shortcut.** Record a system-wide shortcut in Settings → General → Shortcut. LokalBot checks for conflicts with dictation and enabled macOS shortcuts, and pauses the current shortcut while you record a new one. The default remains ⌃⇧ Space.
- **Faster Settings navigation.** Switching Settings categories avoids repeated system login-item queries and unnecessary work in the Ask window. Localized labels also load more efficiently.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/stevyhacker/lokalbot/compare/v0.10.4...v0.10.5
