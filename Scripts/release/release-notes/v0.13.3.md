## What’s new

- **Apple Speech transcription (Mac, macOS 26+).** Choose **Apple Speech** in **Settings → Models** to transcribe with macOS's built-in on-device `SpeechAnalyzer`, with native word timestamps for speaker attribution and vocabulary hints, and no model download from Hugging Face. macOS installs any missing language asset via `AssetInventory`, and no audio or vocabulary leaves your Mac. Covers up to 22 picker languages depending on macOS; Serbian isn't supported. Thanks to blx10.
- **Share a meeting recap for Slack, email or offline HTML (Mac).** A meeting's Share menu adds **Copy recap for Slack / Email** (decisions, open actions with owners and deadlines, open questions and notes) and **Export as HTML…**, which saves a single self-contained `.html` file with light and dark styling and a collapsible transcript. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Lighter first-time setup and 1-click agent setup copy (Mac).** Before the first model download starts, setup lets you switch between **Balanced local** and **Lightweight local** models, and offers **Import past meetings** while models download. **Settings → External Agents** adds **Copy setup snippet…** for Claude Code (MCP command or plugin), Codex plugin, and generic MCP JSON config.
- **Windows installer puts `lokalbot-cli` on `PATH` and supports `/ALLUSERS` (Windows).** `setup.exe` now installs `lokalbot-cli.exe` into `{app}\bin` and adds it to `PATH` (on by default, removed on uninstall), stays per-user without administrator rights by default, and accepts `/ALLUSERS` for machine-wide deploys. Scoop installs are now recognized as package-managed by the updater.

## Improvements

- **Faster Qwen auto-language transcription (Mac).** With language set to Auto, Qwen3-ASR now votes on a 12-window sample across the recording and decodes the track once pinned when at least 90% agree, cutting long single-language track recognition time by roughly 1.25–1.88× with identical transcripts; mixed or borderline tracks fall back to the full-track vote. Croatian, Serbian, Bosnian and Montenegrin tracks with word attribution also skip the throwaway whole-track pass.
- **Faster CLI, MCP and Ask meeting search (all platforms).** A byte-level prefilter skips transcripts and segments that cannot match the query before decoding JSON, speeding up `lokalbot-cli search` and MCP `search_meetings` by roughly 6–10× on rare-word queries at 250 meetings with the same hits. On Windows and Linux, meetings matching the same three or more words now break ties consistently.
- **Faster first autocomplete suggestion after a pause (Mac).** A single-token warm-up runs when you type, click, wake or unlock after 30 seconds idle (never during recordings), keeping the first suggestion after a 5-minute pause around 50 ms instead of 95–130 ms. (A 30-minute pause has not been re-measured yet.)
- **Better action-item ownership and deadlines (Mac).** Distinct meeting actions stay separate when they share context or have different deadlines, deduplication keeps an action's wording, owner, deadline and citation together, and structured deadlines are checked against the cited transcript clause so unsupported dates are cleared instead of carried onto another task.
- **Less idle and main-thread work (all platforms).** Mac launch checks semantic-index freshness off the main thread, Ask and Quick Recall batch screenshot lookups off the main thread, screen search on Mac, Windows and Linux reads recognized text only for kept hits, Windows and Linux Today stops recounting open actions every 30 seconds when nothing changed, and the shared phone meeting list filters and paginates in SQLite (2–4× faster).
- **Fixed:** the Timeline split keeps its pane size when you switch between sessions (Mac).

The new Mac onboarding, sharing, Apple Speech and MCP-copy strings in non-English languages are machine translations. On Windows, the updated installer and Scoop handling were checked in unit tests and hosted Windows CI rather than by hand. Windows and Linux remain in alpha, and the Windows app isn't code-signed. The Android app is on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.dotenv.lokalbot.companion) and here for Android 10 or later, signed with different keys, so one can't update the other. The iPhone and iPad app isn't in the App Store yet.
