## What’s fixed

- **Your commitments keep you as the owner.** Meeting action items now recognize phrases such as “on my side, I’ll…” and “I do plan to…” instead of showing “Owner unclear.” Personal plans are also included when checking for missed action items. Conditions, negation, and speaker identity still determine whether a task belongs to you.
- **Day digests finish while you keep working.** New activity recorded during generation no longer causes the digest to fail.

Summarize an existing meeting again to refresh its generated action owners. Your manual corrections are preserved.
