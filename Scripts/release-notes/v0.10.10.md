## What’s new

- **See what’s happening with each person.** People now shows highlights from your last conversation, shared projects and decisions, and separate lists of work waiting on you or on them. Meetings are grouped by series, and a call recorded in parts counts as one meeting.
- **Keep your people list accurate.** Rename people, merge duplicate entries, undo a merge, or mark an entry as Not a Person. These corrections also apply in Projects and the CLI/MCP.
- **Track the projects that matter to you.** Add projects, give them alternate names, merge duplicates, and hide irrelevant suggestions. Project pages find more relevant meetings by checking full summaries and transcripts, with links to the matching moments.
- **Get a quick “Where it stands” note.** Tracked projects can summarize recent evidence into an overview, points linked to their source meetings, and open questions. Notes are marked out of date when the evidence changes and removed when a source meeting is deleted.
- **Make open work easier to follow.** Project actions are split into Your Actions and Waiting on Others. People flags older items with Still open?, and phrases such as “later today” and “this afternoon” now resolve to the day they were said, including supported Serbian phrases.

After updating, Projects starts with suggestions you can choose to track. “Where it stands” uses your selected Think model, including an approved remote provider when configured. Opening a tracked project can refresh its note automatically when that model is ready.
