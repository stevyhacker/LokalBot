## What’s new

- **Files (Mac).** Search the folders you choose by what is in them: documents, PDF pages, pictures, screenshots, and moments in recordings and video. Turn it on in Settings → Files; everything stays on your Mac. [How it works](https://www.lokalbot.com/explainers/files-search.pdf)
- **Screens by what they showed (Mac).** Screen search also matches a screen by its picture, not only its text.
- **Settings by feature (Mac).** Settings opens on an Overview of every feature with one-click setup, and each feature has one page.
- **One switch for external agents (Mac).** Choose what agents can read in Settings → External Agents, now including Files.
