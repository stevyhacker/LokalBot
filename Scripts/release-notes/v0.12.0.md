## What’s new

- **Files: search your folders by what is in them (Mac).** Choose folders in Settings → Files, and Ask, Quick Recall and the Ask assistant find a document, a PDF page, a picture or a screenshot by what it says or shows, not only by its name. Recordings and videos are found at the moment that matches and play from there in a LokalBot window, and the assistant can listen to that moment to answer you. Before a folder is read, a sheet shows what it holds, how long its first read takes on your Mac and how big the index gets. Reading runs in the background, slower while you work, pauses while a meeting records, in Low Power Mode and when the Mac is warm, and waits for a charger on battery. Your files and the index stay on your Mac; the image model downloads once (555 MB). Files is built on DigUp’s engine (MIT). [How it works](https://www.lokalbot.com/explainers/files-search.pdf)
- **Find screens by what they showed (Mac).** With screen context set to Text + visual context and search by meaning on, screen search also matches a screen by its picture, not only by its text.
- **Settings by feature, with an Overview (Mac).** Settings opens on Overview: every feature, whether it’s on, and a one-click Turn On, Try, Connect or Set Up, plus a Needs Attention list for missing permissions and learned items to review. Each feature now has one page with everything that turns it on and sets it up, and model pickers open over the page instead of sending you elsewhere. General sits right under Overview, and the other pages are grouped under Memory, Assistant, Writing, Connections and App, with new pages for Files, Email, Ask, Daily Review and External Agents. Links from Ask, Today and Agent land on the right page, and `lokalbot-cli config` categories follow the new pages.
- **One switch for agents reading your data (Mac).** Settings → External Agents has one switch, Allow external agents to read your LokalBot data, with a box for each kind: Meetings, Screen memory, What I shared about myself, and Files. With Files ticked, agents such as Claude Code and Codex can search the folders you chose with `lokalbot-cli search-files` or the MCP tool `search_files`. Updating widens nothing: what you allowed before shows as it was, and Files starts off.

## Improvements

- **Faster and lighter (Mac).** Screen search by meaning answers in about 11 ms instead of 70–80 ms, and opening the screen and search databases at launch takes milliseconds instead of most of a second. Keyword screen search, the Ask assistant’s screen search and the daily cleanup of old captures are several times faster. Captures that differ only by a blinking cursor are no longer stored again, reading windows for screen context costs less, the speaker model unloads when it isn’t needed, and typing in Ask or in meeting notes no longer redraws the rest of the app. Coding-agent transcripts are read only where they grew, not in full every 10 minutes.
- **Less disk used by updates (Mac).** Each update used to leave another copy of the bundled runtimes behind, about 50 MB each; one Mac had 126 of them (5.9 GB). LokalBot now removes the versions no running copy of the app uses.

Windows and Linux remain in alpha, with no changes in this release beyond translations. The Windows app is not yet code-signed.
