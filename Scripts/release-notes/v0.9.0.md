## What’s new

- **Your action items come first again.** Commitments you make on your microphone and requests you answer are listed first as yours, ahead of other participants' follow-ups. A new **My microphone is me** setting (on by default) can be turned off when several people share one microphone. Remote speech echoed into your microphone is not treated as yours. Summarize an existing meeting again to refresh its action items.
- **Today shows the rest of your day.** The next meeting keeps Join and Record with its preparation context open, and later meetings are listed below it. Personal appointments without other attendees or a meeting link no longer take a meeting's place; solo events titled like meetings are listed without Record.
- **Simpler workspaces.** Today, Ask, Timeline, meetings, and Settings were reorganized: Ask searches as you type with its composer docked at the bottom, Writing tools (autocomplete and dictation) live in Settings, the Timeline session rail is resizable, and Settings are grouped into clearer sections. New users see the Getting Started card on Today again until they dismiss it.
- **Meet recordings stop when the call ends.** A Google Meet recording no longer ends mid-call when Chrome's call controls can't be read while the call's tab is still open, and it starts again right away if the call is picked up again. Recordings you start yourself from the menu bar or Today now stop when the call ends too.
- **Google Meet speaker naming removed.** LokalBot no longer reads participant names or speaking activity from Chrome during recordings. Remembered voices now come only from your microphone; existing remembered people are kept.
- **Opening another meeting while recording no longer crashes LokalBot.**
- **Nemotron 3 speaker separation.** New installs separate voices with Nemotron 3 (Preview); existing installs keep their current speaker model, and Pyannote Community-1 remains available.
- **Safer privacy, recording, and model handling.** Scheduled remote summaries need their own approval, library migration failures open a recovery window instead of starting normally, capture and retention edge cases are tightened, and speech and runtime model files are verified before use. Files that fail verification must be downloaded or repaired again.
