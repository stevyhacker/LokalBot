## What’s new

- **Recent dictations.** A dictation that finishes after you’ve moved to another app is copied to the clipboard instead of pasted, and your next copy used to replace it for good. LokalBot now keeps your last 10 dictations for 7 days, encrypted on your computer, so you can copy them again: from **Last dictation** in the menu bar, the **Recent dictations** list on the Dictation page, or **Copy last dictation** in ⌘K. On Windows and Linux, use **Copy last dictation** in the tray or **Settings → Dictation**. Text meant for a password field and cancelled dictations are never kept, and **Clear** deletes the list.
- **Speakers you name are recognized in later meetings.** Every name you save now teaches LokalBot that voice, including people who joined by call. In new meetings, a call speaker whose voice clearly matches someone you named is named automatically. For meetings from the last 14 days, the Summary tab lists the **Recognized voices** with **Apply Names**. Names you gave before this update are learned from your saved meetings. The **Remember speakers on this Mac** setting is gone; **Manage remembered people…** in Settings still lets you rename or forget anyone.
- **Personal Context.** “About you” is now **Personal Context**, with its own Settings page. It now learns from what you said in meetings, not only from what you typed, and reads each day once, so its suggestions no longer disappear before you review them.
- **Linux updates with apt and dnf.** Installing the `.deb`, or the `.rpm` on Fedora, now also adds the LokalBot repository at `pkg.lokalbot.com`, so `sudo apt upgrade` or `sudo dnf upgrade` brings later versions.

## Fixes

- **Autocomplete in Chrome, Arc, Brave and Edge rich-text fields** (a Reddit post, for example) starts at the right place after the paragraphs above it, matches the field’s text size, and accepts with Tab. LokalBot asks these browsers for their full accessibility mode to read the caret and font; you can turn that off in **Settings → Writing → Advanced → Use enhanced accessibility in Chrome-based browsers**.
- Autocomplete suggestions are readable in dark fields after you’ve used a light page in the same app. The suggestion color is now measured per field instead of per app.
- Past days’ digests and overnight reviews are no longer rebuilt again and again while coding-agent sessions continue, which spent model calls each time.
- Updated llama.cpp to v0.7.0, the speech runtimes (sherpa-onnx 1.13.8, WhisperKit 1.1.1, FluidAudio 0.17.7), and Agent Mode’s runtime to pi 1.3.0. Agent Mode asks you to download its runtime once more after updating.
