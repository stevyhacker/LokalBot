# LokalBot Support

Start with the [README](README.md), the [Windows and Linux guide](LinuxWindows/README.md),
and the [release guide](RELEASING.md). For a bug,
question, or feature request, open a GitHub issue:

https://github.com/stevyhacker/lokalbot/issues/new/choose

Include the LokalBot version, your OS and its version (on Linux, also the
distribution, desktop, and X11 or Wayland), your Mac model or your PC's CPU,
GPU and RAM, the exact action that failed, and the smallest reproducible steps.
You may attach a redacted excerpt from LokalBot's log (`debug.log` in
`~/Library/Application Support/me.dotenv.LokalBot/` on a Mac; the library's
`logs` folder on Windows and Linux, under Settings → Advanced → Logs), but never
post meeting audio, transcripts, API keys, tokens, model-server credentials, or
other private material.

For a suspected security vulnerability, do not open a public issue. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

This is a community open-source project; response times are not guaranteed.
