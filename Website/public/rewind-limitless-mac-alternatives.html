Mac recall

Best Rewind AI and Limitless Mac alternatives after the Meta shutdown

After Meta acquired Limitless in December 2025, the Rewind / Limitless Mac app disabled screen and audio capture on December 19, 2025, and Limitless stopped selling its Pendant. If you relied on local screen search and bot-free call recall on macOS, here are the active local replacements and how they differ.

Updated November 26, 2026 · 6 min read

What happened to Rewind and Limitless on Mac

Rewind introduced searchable local screen history and meeting recall on Apple Silicon before rebranding to Limitless and focusing on its wearable Pendant. Following Meta's acquisition in December 2025, a final Mac update disabled screen and audio capture, Pendant sales stopped, and the old rewind.ai domain moved on. Anyone wanting local workday recall on macOS now needs an actively maintained replacement with clear data ownership so the same shutdown cannot lock away their history again.

LokalBot: opt-in encrypted Mac timeline, screen search, and local meetings

GPLv3 · Free · Apple Silicon Mac + Windows/Linux alpha + E2EE mobile companion

LokalBot picks up the core Rewind workflow without continuous video bloat or vendor lock-in. Out of the box, it logs app and window activity on a color-coded day timeline. Turning on screen memory adds Apple Vision OCR (120 ms synthetic / 243 ms real screenshots, 0.971 F1) and AES-GCM encrypted screenshots searchable by keywords and visual meaning via EmbeddingGemma 2, automatically pruned after 14 days by default. Pair that with bot-free meeting notes, local Ask, 56 ms autocomplete, and read-only MCP for Claude Code and Cursor. See LokalBot vs Rewind / Limitless.

Screenpipe: continuous 24/7 capture for developer automations

Source-available commercial license · macOS (ARM & Intel), Windows, Linux

Screenpipe is closest to Rewind's always-on capture model, storing roughly 30 GB per month of continuous screen and audio context and exposing a local REST API and MCP server. It fits developers scripting custom pipes over raw capture who accept a source-available commercial license for work ($21/mo Basic, $42/seat Business). See LokalBot vs Screenpipe.

Meetings-only replacements (Meetily, Anarlog, MacWhisper, Granola)

Use these if you only needed Rewind for call transcripts

If you never used Rewind's screen timeline and only cared about bot-free meeting summaries, dedicated notetakers such as Meetily, Anarlog, MacWhisper, or Granola cover meetings without screen permissions. Compare the full local field in Best local AI meeting note takers.

How to avoid another acquisition shutdown

  • Pick OSI open source (GPLv3 or MIT): Even if a maintainer stops tomorrow, the source code and your local build remain runnable.
  • Verify offline operation: Confirm that transcription, OCR, and search run without an activation server or login token.
  • Check plain-file exports: Automatic Markdown and SQLite ownership ensures you can move your meeting archive to Obsidian or Git at any time.

FAQ

Questions about this guide

Is LokalBot an exact video-scrubber clone of Rewind?

No. Rewind recorded continuous compressed screen video so you could scrub frame by frame. LokalBot focuses on searchable activity, opt-in OCR text, AES-GCM encrypted periodic screenshots (14-day default retention), and full bot-free meeting notes. You get fast keyword and semantic search across what you saw and heard without massive video archives.

Can LokalBot run if I also switch between Mac and Windows or Linux?

Yes. LokalBot uses the same local SQLite library format across its Apple Silicon Mac app and its Windows and Linux (alpha) Rust + GPUI app.

What if I want to replace a Limitless Pendant for in-person meetings?

LokalBot offers free iOS and Android companion apps that record in-person audio and sync end-to-end encrypted over LAN or Tailscale straight to your computer for local transcription, with zero cloud subscription. See LokalBot vs Plaud for details.
