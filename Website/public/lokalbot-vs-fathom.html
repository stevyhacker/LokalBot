LokalBot vs Fathom

Fathom is popular for a reason: its free plan includes unlimited basic recordings and transcripts on Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams, and paid tiers add shared team folders and CRM sync for HubSpot and Salesforce. The trade-off is that your calls live in Fathom's cloud under an account, advanced AI summaries cap at five per month on the free tier, and most platforms still send a bot into the meeting. LokalBot captures from your computer's own audio with no bot, runs transcription and unlimited summaries locally, and needs no account.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Fathom if…

Strong for sales and customer-facing teams living in a CRM.

  • You want call notes and deal fields synced directly into HubSpot or Salesforce
  • Your team wants shared call playlists, comments, and coaching clips
  • You want video call recordings hosted in the cloud for easy link sharing
  • You need a vendor-managed HIPAA or SSO/SCIM workspace

Pick LokalBot if…

Built for unlimited local notes without a bot or a cloud library.

  • You want unlimited AI summaries, action items, and search with no monthly cap
  • Your calls cannot be uploaded to a third-party cloud — legal, medical, finance, or NDA work
  • You want zero bots joining your Zoom, Meet, Teams, or local calls on Mac, Windows, or Linux
  • You want dictation, a day timeline, autocomplete, and local MCP/CLI access in one open-source app

Questions

The short answers.

Can I bring my existing Fathom meetings into LokalBot?

Yes. LokalBot includes a built-in Fathom importer (transcripts, summaries, downloaded recordings, or Fathom API JSON), or you can convert Fathom exports into Obsidian Markdown offline in your browser with the free Meeting Migrator.

Does Fathom work without a bot?

Fathom offers a bot-free capture option in beta on Mac, while Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams otherwise use the Fathom notetaker bot. Even without the bot, Fathom uploads and stores recordings and transcripts in its cloud. LokalBot processes audio, transcripts, and summaries locally by default.

Why is LokalBot free when Fathom charges $15–$34 per seat?

LokalBot has no cloud transcription or LLM servers to pay for — the models run on your own Mac, Windows, or Linux computer. You pay for your own hardware and electricity, never a per-seat subscription.

Will LokalBot work on apps besides Zoom, Meet, and Teams?

Yes. Because LokalBot captures local system audio rather than relying on platform bot APIs, it works with Slack huddles, Discord, Webex, FaceTime, WhatsApp, and any other call app on your computer.
