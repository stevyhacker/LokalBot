LokalBot vs Read.ai

Read.ai connects to your calendar and dispatches the Read Assistant bot into Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet calls, adding engagement scores, coaching reports, and cross-tool search in its cloud. That bot-first model is also why many IT teams block Read.ai and why callers ask who invited the extra participant. LokalBot never joins a call, never emails attendees behind your back, runs transcription and summaries locally on your computer, and has no monthly meeting limit.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Read.ai if…

Built for cloud analytics, coaching scores, and cross-app team search.

  • You want speaker engagement metrics, talk-time coaching, and video highlight reels
  • You want one cloud search index across Slack, email, CRM, and team calls
  • You want a bot that records calls even when your laptop is closed
  • Your organization buys Enterprise+ for centralized SSO, domain capture, and BAA compliance

Pick LokalBot if…

Built for quiet, local notes that never ambush a call.

  • Your company or clients block Read.ai and other third-party meeting bots
  • You never want a bot auto-joining a sensitive 1:1 or emailing recaps to external participants
  • You want unlimited local transcripts and summaries for free instead of a 5-meeting cap
  • You want your audio and notes kept strictly on your own computer under open-source GPLv3 code

Questions

The short answers.

Why do companies block Read.ai, and how is LokalBot different?

Most IT policies block third-party meeting bots because they join as an external cloud participant and upload confidential audio to outside servers. LokalBot runs on your computer with local models and no cloud backend — see our guide My company blocks AI notetaker bots for an IT review checklist. Always follow your organization's recording and consent rules.

Will LokalBot ever auto-email meeting recaps to attendees?

No. LokalBot has no account, no email server, and no cloud sharing backend. Everything stays in your local library until you deliberately export or copy it.

Does LokalBot cap how many meetings I can record per month?

No. Unlike Read.ai's 5-meeting free tier, LokalBot has no minute or meeting caps — storage is limited only by your own disk.

How does speaker separation work without a bot?

LokalBot records your microphone and the meeting app's audio on separate tracks and runs local speaker diarization (Nemotron 3 Core ML on Mac, pyannote + TitaNet on Windows/Linux). Capture-track labels describe where audio came from rather than verified speaker identity, and you can name speakers locally.
