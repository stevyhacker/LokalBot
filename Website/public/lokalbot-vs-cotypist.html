LokalBot vs Cotypist

Cotypist (cotypist.app) is a polished proprietary macOS app that brings local, offline ghost-text autocomplete to Apple Silicon using on-device Gemma and Qwen models, with a 100-word daily free cap and Plus/Pro subscriptions (~$72-$108/yr). LokalBot ships free, unlimited open-source inline autocomplete across macOS, Windows, and Linux (alpha) using our Apache-2.0 fine-tuned Gemma 4 E2B (LokalBot v4) model (56 ms typing median, 20.2 ms boundary median, 49-53 ms after a 5-minute pause, and 56/56 context grounding), plus optional grounding in your own meeting notes.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Cotypist if…

A focused commercial Mac utility for dedicated writing autocomplete.

  • You want a standalone macOS autocomplete utility with zero meeting or timeline features
  • You want Pro-tier access to larger local models (like Gemma 4 26B A4B) and per-app style rules
  • You want built-in autocorrect and mid-line edits tuned specifically for macOS 14+

Pick LokalBot if…

Free, open-source 56 ms autocomplete grounded in your real work.

  • You want unlimited local autocomplete with no 100-word daily cap or yearly subscription
  • You want completions that can bring in facts from your meetings (56/56 context grounding)
  • You also use Windows or Linux (alpha), or want meetings, dictation, and MCP sharing one RAM budget
  • You want verifiable open-source code and open Apache-2.0 model weights

Questions

The short answers.

How fast is LokalBot's autocomplete compared to Cotypist?

In our reproducible headless keystroke benchmark on an Apple M4 Max, Gemma 4 E2B runs at 56 ms median while typing (20.2 ms median boundary latency and 80.0 ms p95 on LokalBot v4) and 49-53 ms on the first suggestion after a 5-minute idle pause. The Lightweight preset (LFM2.5 1.2B, 0.73 GB) hits 26 ms median. Full scripts and logs are on the benchmarks page.

Does LokalBot send keystrokes to a cloud server?

No. Built-in autocomplete runs entirely in-process via llama.cpp on your computer, never reads password or credential fields, ignores private browser windows, and lets you exclude any app in Settings.

Does LokalBot autocomplete work on Windows and Linux too?

Yes. The native Rust + GPUI app (alpha) runs the same Gemma 4 E2B and LFM2.5 1.2B models on Windows (via UI Automation) and Linux (X11, plus Wayland input-method support on Sway, Hyprland, GNOME, and KDE Plasma).
