LokalBot vs Notion AI

Notion AI Meeting Notes brings bot-free transcription right inside your workspace: type /meet in the Notion desktop app and your summary lands beside your team's docs and databases. The catch is that full access requires Notion's Business plan ($20/user/month billed annually for every workspace member), audio goes to cloud AI subprocessors (with zero data retention limited to Enterprise), and there is no Linux app. LokalBot gives you bot-free capture on your computer for free, runs speech and summary models locally, works offline, and exports clean Markdown anywhere.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Notion AI if…

Hard to beat if your whole company already runs on Notion Business.

  • Your team already pays for Notion Business or Enterprise and keeps every project in Notion databases
  • You want meeting summaries automatically linked into shared Notion wikis and team Q&A
  • You want collaborative live editing inside the same note during calls
  • You need Enterprise SSO, workspace permissions, and a vendor BAA

Pick LokalBot if…

Built for people who want local ownership without a $20/seat workspace upgrade.

  • You don't want to upgrade every seat in your Notion workspace to Business just for meeting notes
  • Your calls can't be sent to cloud subprocessors — you want real on-device transcription and summaries
  • You prefer plain Markdown files (Obsidian, Logseq, Git) or local CLI/MCP access for Claude Code and Codex
  • You also use Linux, or want dictation, a day timeline, and system-wide autocomplete in one free app

Questions

The short answers.

Can I still paste LokalBot summaries into Notion?

Yes. Open any meeting and choose Share → Copy as Markdown (or Export as Markdown), then paste straight into a Notion page — your audio and raw transcript still never touch a cloud server.

Is Notion AI Meeting Notes included in Notion Free or Plus?

No. Notion folded full AI Meeting Notes into the Business ($20/user/month billed annually) and Enterprise tiers; Free and Plus accounts only receive a limited trial. LokalBot has no trial limits or per-seat pricing.

Does Notion AI Meeting Notes run locally on my Mac?

No. While the Notion desktop app captures local system audio so no bot joins the call, it sends the audio to cloud AI subprocessors to transcribe and summarize. LokalBot runs both speech recognition and summarization on your own hardware by default.

How does LokalBot compare for local AI coding agents?

LokalBot exposes a read-only local CLI and MCP server (off until enabled in Settings) so Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor can search your meetings and action items directly on your machine without cloud tokens or workspace permissions.
