LokalBot vs Jamie

Jamie (meetjamie.ai) is a popular European bot-free AI meeting assistant. It captures device audio on Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android, deletes raw audio after transcription, and hosts notes on Frankfurt servers with ISO 27001 certification. Even with EU hosting, your audio and transcripts still cross third-party cloud servers under a paid account. LokalBot keeps bot-free capture, transcription, and summaries strictly on your computer with zero cloud subprocessors, unlimited meetings, Linux support, and GPLv3 source.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Jamie if…

Built for European teams wanting managed EU cloud sync and CRM connectors.

  • You want ISO 27001 vendor certification, shared team templates, and managed workspaces
  • You want direct sync into HubSpot, Salesforce, Asana, or Notion without local scripts
  • You want transcription in 100+ languages with zero local model downloads
  • You need a managed DPA with EU data residency instead of local device storage

Pick LokalBot if…

Built for zero-subprocessor GDPR compliance on your own hardware.

  • You want zero bytes of meeting audio or text leaving your computer for any cloud server
  • You want unlimited meetings and duration with no EUR 24-47/mo subscription
  • You also use Linux, Obsidian Markdown export, or local read-only MCP for coding agents
  • You want GPLv3 code your security team or DPO can inspect directly

Questions

The short answers.

How is LokalBot different from Jamie for GDPR?

Jamie reduces transfer risk by using EU servers in Frankfurt and deleting raw audio after transcription, but Jamie remains a third-party cloud processor that receives your meeting audio and keeps transcripts in your cloud account. With LokalBot's built-in models, zero meeting data leaves your machine, removing cloud subprocessors entirely. With either tool, you still must inform participants and establish a valid legal basis before recording.

Does Jamie work on Linux?

No. Jamie offers macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android apps. LokalBot runs natively on Apple Silicon Macs plus Windows and Linux (alpha) in Rust + GPUI, alongside iOS and Android companion apps that sync over LAN or Tailscale.

Why is LokalBot free while Jamie Pro costs around EUR 47 a month?

Jamie pays for cloud GPU transcription, LLM inference, and EU server hosting. LokalBot runs Apache-2.0 fine-tuned speech and language models directly on your computer, so there is no cloud bill to pass on.
