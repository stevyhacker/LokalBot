Benchmark

Local speech-to-text vs. cloud APIs on real meetings

No closed cloud speech-to-text API measurably beat LokalBot's local Qwen3-ASR 1.7B on real two-track meetings—and several cloud APIs failed in ways that are hard to catch in clean benchmarks.

November 18, 2026 · 6 min read

Why test against cloud APIs

People often assume running transcription on a laptop means trading away accuracy for privacy. We tested LokalBot's default local models—Qwen3-ASR 1.7B (8-bit) and Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 on an Apple M4 Max—head-to-head against direct API calls to OpenAI (gpt-transcribe, gpt-4o-transcribe, gpt-4o-mini-transcribe), Google (gemini-3.5-transcribe, gemini-3.8-flash), and Alibaba Model Studio (qwen-audio-3.1-asr-flash, qwen3-asr-flash).

Two-track real meetings: where cloud APIs broke

On 99 two-track chunks (69.9 minutes) from real meetings scored by cross-family ROVER consensus disagreement (measuring agreement, not human ground truth), local Qwen3-ASR 1.7B placed first at 4.2% disagreement (0.4 s median latency, 0 dropped spans), tied within confidence intervals with Gemini 3.5 Transcribe (4.5%, 6.3 s latency) and Qwen-Audio 3.1 ASR Flash (4.6%, 2.9 s latency).

Two failure modes jumped out immediately:

  • Gemini 3.8 Flash invented speech on quiet clips. Despite leading AMI WER by 0.9 points, on real meeting chunks it hallucinated 115 words across 15 spans—including turning a 1.2-second clip that every other system heard as one word or silence into a fluent 30-word sentence.
  • GPT-4o Transcribe silently dropped microphone speech. It logged 278 dropped words in runs of four or more and 42% disagreement on one meeting's microphone track (vs. 7–14% for the other systems). Only ~20% of those dropped words appeared on the system track, ruling out echo suppression.
  • Span length matters inside our own app. The test also caught a bug in LokalBot's older production path: cutting audio at speaker turns before transcription hurt AMI WER (26.5% vs. 17.7%). Assigning speakers after transcribing longer spans closed that gap.

Data and reproduction

Full tables, ROVER methodology, and caveats are in Benchmarks/transcribe/CloudSTT/results/2026-09-30/REPORT.md and the follow-up span-length report. Try the same local models offline in LokalBot.
