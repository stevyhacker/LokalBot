  • Granola: Open Account Settings, choose Export Data (CSV or JSON), or copy any note transcript and use Paste text.

Frequently asked questions

      

        Are my meeting files uploaded to a server?
        
No. Every file, including ZIP archives and Microsoft Teams DOCX transcripts, is unpacked, parsed, and packaged into Markdown or ZIP strictly inside your browser tab. You can turn off Wi-Fi or inspect the Network tab in DevTools to verify zero requests are made.

      

      

        Which meeting export formats work?
        
Bulk .zip archives, Microsoft Teams .docx and .vtt files, CSV (including Granola and Wispr Flow exports), JSON (Granola, Fireflies, Otter, Fathom, Screenpipe, Krisp, Tactiq, Wispr Flow), Markdown, SubRip (.srt), and plain text (.txt) or pasted transcripts. To import full recordings and searchable history into LokalBot itself, choose Import Meetings From under the Recording menu inside the app.

      

      

        Does the exported Markdown work with obsidian-lokalbot?
        
Yes. When Obsidian + LokalBot format is enabled, every exported file writes YAML frontmatter matching LokalBot's native exporter (people:, projects:, tags: [lokalbot/meeting], and source: LokalBot) so the LokalBot plugin for Obsidian picks up migrated notes immediately.

      

      

        How do I automate future calls straight into Obsidian?
        
Install LokalBot on macOS, Windows, or Linux, open Settings, go to Meetings, and enable Meeting note export with format set to Obsidian. You can also install the LokalBot plugin for Obsidian to browse, search, sync, and ask your local meetings inside Obsidian.
