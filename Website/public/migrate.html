Record future calls locally with no bot — straight into Obsidian.

      
LokalBot is a free, open-source (GPLv3) desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux. It records both sides of Zoom, Teams, Meet, and Slack calls locally with no meeting bot, imports your existing Granola, Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, Zoom, Voice Memos, and MacWhisper history, and writes Markdown notes straight into your Obsidian vault. Pair it with the open-source LokalBot Obsidian plugin to search, sync, and ask your meetings without leaving Obsidian.

Frequently asked questions

      

        Are my meeting files uploaded to a server?
        
No. Every file is read, parsed, and packaged into Markdown or a ZIP archive strictly inside your browser tab using plain JavaScript. You can turn off Wi-Fi or inspect the Network tab in DevTools to verify zero requests are made.

      

      

        Which meeting export formats work?
        
CSV (including Granola's historical data export), JSON, Markdown, WebVTT (.vtt), SubRip (.srt), and plain text (.txt) exports from Granola, Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, Zoom, and most transcript tools. If you want to import full recordings and searchable history into LokalBot itself, use Recording → Import Meetings From… inside the app.

      

      

        How do I automate future calls straight into Obsidian?
        
Install LokalBot on macOS, Windows, or Linux and enable Settings → Meetings → Meeting note export with format set to Obsidian, or install the LokalBot plugin for Obsidian to browse, search, sync, and ask your local meetings inside Obsidian.
