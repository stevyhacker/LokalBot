LokalBot vs tl;dv

tl;dv is built for sales, customer success, and product teams that want recorded video reels, CRM auto-fill, and multi-call coaching dashboards. Its virtual bot joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls, stores video in the cloud (with a 3-month storage cap and ~10 AI summaries/mo on Free), and scales up to $59-$98/user/mo for sales intelligence. LokalBot skips the bot completely, runs speech and summary models locally on your computer, keeps unlimited history on your disk, and costs nothing.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick tl;dv if…

Purpose-built for revenue teams sharing video clips and CRM playbooks.

  • You want shareable video highlight reels from customer calls
  • Your sales team needs rep scorecards, objection tracking, and automated HubSpot/Salesforce updates
  • You work in a browser and want cloud-hosted video archives across a sales org
  • You need SOC 2 and centralized Enterprise admin controls

Pick LokalBot if…

Built for quiet, permanent local notes with no bots and no seat fees.

  • You do not want a tl;dv bot entering sensitive client, engineering, or 1:1 calls
  • You want unlimited local summaries and history that never expires after 3 months
  • You want calls outside Zoom/Meet/Teams (Slack huddles, Discord, FaceTime, Webex)
  • You want local dictation, autocomplete, Obsidian export, and free local MCP

Questions

The short answers.

Does LokalBot record video like tl;dv?

No. LokalBot records two synchronized audio tracks (microphone and meeting app) plus optional opt-in screen text/screenshots on your timeline, keeping storage tiny compared to cloud video recordings.

Why do teams switch from tl;dv to a local recorder?

Two common reasons: clients or IT block visible meeting bots from joining calls, and individuals do not want to pay $18-$98 per seat per month or lose recordings after the 3-month free storage window.

Can LokalBot work completely offline?

Yes. After a one-time download of the open Apache-2.0 models, LokalBot records, transcribes, summarizes, and searches offline with zero cloud servers.
