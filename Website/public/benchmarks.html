Submit a model or hardware run

Model creators, quantizers, and community members are welcome to test new models or fill missing hardware profiles (especially M1 / M2 / M3 16 GB, Linux Vulkan/CUDA, and Windows).

  1. Pick a harness under Benchmarks/ (speech, diarization, memory restraint, autocomplete, QMSum retrieval, extraction, or OCR) and keep the frozen dataset and scorer unchanged.

Radical Honesty

What has not been measured yet

Every score here reflects a specific harness and machine, not a universal ranking. Known gaps we still want to close:

  • M1, M2, and M3 Macs (especially 16 GB): Nearly all Apple Silicon timings are from one M4 Max (48 GB).
  • Windows & Linux Vulkan/CUDA/CPU stack: Only memory restraint and Spark-X2.6 ran on Linux/Windows so far; full sherpa-onnx ASR and GPUI Vulkan/CUDA timings are pending.
  • 30-minute idle pauses under RAM pressure: The 5-minute autocomplete warm-up fix is measured (49–53 ms), not 30-minute OS file paging.
  • Blind human summary grading: Extraction suites check structured fields and tools, not blinded human ratings of full meeting recaps.
  • 5–8 speaker calls & noisy multilingual rooms: Diarization covers 4-speaker English AMI rooms; non-English ASR slices are small FLEURS sets.

FAQ

Methodology & common questions

What hardware were these benchmarks measured on?

Primary desktop runs used an Apple M4 Max MacBook Pro with 48 GB of unified memory. The memory-restraint and Spark-X2.6 suites ran on an Ubuntu Linux AMD Ryzen 7 9700X host (64 GB RAM), and the community-suggested MemT-1.0-Nano extension ran with experimental NF4 expert offload on Windows with a Radeon RX 6600 XT. M1, M2, and M3 Macs have not been measured yet.

How did local Qwen3-ASR 1.7B compare with closed speech-to-text APIs?

On 27.4 minutes of overlapped AMI Mix-Headset audio, local Qwen3-ASR 1.7B scored 17.6% WER at 44× realtime and $0/hr, ahead of GPT-4o Transcribe (23.0%), GPT Transcribe (22.2%), GPT-4o mini Transcribe (21.8%), and Alibaba's cloud Qwen APIs, and within confidence intervals of Gemini 3.8 Flash (16.7%). On three real two-track meetings, Qwen3-ASR 1.7B had the lowest consensus disagreement (4.2%), while Gemini 3.8 Flash invented 115 words on quiet clips and GPT-4o Transcribe dropped 278 words.

Why do memory benchmarks score both recall and restraint?

A personal memory writer that saves every sentence can reach 100% recall while polluting a user's profile with temporary tasks, third-party details, and excluded sensitive facts. Our 120-case suite scores eligible recall alongside unwanted proposals and empty cases correctly left blank so tradeoffs stay visible.

Can I reproduce these results or submit a new model or chip run?

Yes. Every suite links directly to its harness, frozen cases, pinned model hashes, and raw results in the Benchmarks folder on GitHub, and all aggregated rows are served as machine-readable JSON at lokalbot.com/benchmarks.json. Open a pull request with a new model or hardware run (such as M1–M3, Linux Vulkan/CUDA, or Windows) or file an issue to suggest a model.
