LokalBot vs Tactiq

Tactiq avoids meeting bots by running as a Chrome extension that captures live captions inside Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams tabs, then sending that text to cloud AI APIs for summaries. That makes installation quick in Chrome, but it ties transcript quality to the call platform's built-in captions, struggles with native desktop apps outside the browser, and meters AI summaries behind monthly credit caps. LokalBot is a native app that records real two-track audio from any desktop call app and transcribes and summarizes locally with no credit meter.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Tactiq if…

Handy if every meeting runs in Chrome and you cannot install desktop software.

  • Your laptop is locked down to Chrome extensions and blocks native app installs
  • You specifically want zero audio files saved anywhere, only caption text
  • Every call you attend uses Google Meet, Zoom web, or Teams with live captions enabled
  • Your company wants a vendor with SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and SAML SSO

Pick LokalBot if…

Built for native audio accuracy, local privacy, and unlimited summaries.

  • You take calls in native Zoom, Teams, Slack, FaceTime, or browsers other than Chrome
  • You want full local audio replay and high-accuracy on-device speech models (52 languages)
  • You want unlimited AI summaries and free local MCP instead of paying $20-$40/mo
  • You want transcripts kept off cloud AI APIs and exported straight to Obsidian Markdown

Questions

The short answers.

Why does capturing real audio matter compared to browser captions?

Live platform captions can drop technical terms, struggle with non-English or bilingual speech, and are disabled by some hosts. Recording two local audio tracks lets LokalBot run fine-tuned Qwen3-ASR locally, support custom vocabulary, replay the exact moment someone spoke, and re-transcribe later with a better model.

Does Tactiq include MCP on the free or Pro plan?

Tactiq's MCP and Claude connector sit on its Business plan ($29.17/user/mo annual, $40 monthly). LokalBot includes a free, read-only local stdio MCP server for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Gemini CLI with no cloud account.

Can I bring existing Tactiq transcripts into Obsidian?

Yes. Drop Tactiq text or JSON exports into the free offline Meeting Migrator in your browser to convert them into Obsidian Markdown with zero uploads.
