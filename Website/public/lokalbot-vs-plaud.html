LokalBot vs Plaud

Plaud combines dedicated recording hardware (Plaud Note at ~$159, NotePin at ~$159-$179, or Note Pro at ~$189) with Plaud Desktop and mobile apps backed by cloud transcription and 10,000+ summary templates. Plaud Desktop requires a bound hardware device, and heavy users pay $99.99/yr (Pro, 1,200 min/mo) or $239.99/yr (Unlimited) for cloud minutes. LokalBot requires zero extra hardware: your Mac, Windows, or Linux computer records virtual calls locally, and the free iOS and Android companion apps record in-person meetings and sync end-to-end encrypted over LAN or Tailscale to your computer for local transcription.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Plaud if…

Great hardware if you record cellular phone calls or want a one-button gadget.

  • You want a MagSafe physical recorder that captures regular cellular phone calls via vibration sensor
  • You want a wearable clip-on (NotePin) for all-day in-person recording without pulling out your phone
  • You prefer cloud transcription across 112+ languages with thousands of hosted summary templates
  • You do not want to run local models on a laptop

Pick LokalBot if…

Zero extra hardware, zero cloud subscription, end-to-end encrypted sync.

  • You do not want to buy a $159-$189 gadget plus a $100-$240/yr cloud minute plan
  • Your recordings cannot leave your own devices for a vendor cloud
  • You want free iOS and Android companion recording that syncs E2EE straight to your Mac, Windows, or Linux PC
  • You want local search, Obsidian export, dictation, autocomplete, and read-only MCP in one GPLv3 app

Questions

The short answers.

How does LokalBot's mobile companion work without a cloud server?

Pair the free iPhone, iPad, or Android companion app with LokalBot on your Mac, Windows, or Linux computer by scanning a QR code. Recordings, notes, actions, Timeline, and Day Digests sync directly over your local Wi-Fi or Tailscale using end-to-end encryption, with no LokalBot server in the middle.

Can LokalBot record regular cellular phone calls like Plaud Note?

On iPhone and Android, OS sandboxing prevents third-party apps from directly tapping cellular voice lines, which is where Plaud Note's hardware vibration sensor helps. LokalBot's mobile companion records in-person conversations and speakerphone audio, while the desktop app captures both sides of FaceTime, Zoom, Teams, Meet, and WhatsApp Desktop calls locally.

Does LokalBot cap transcription minutes per month?

No. Because transcription and summaries run on your own computer with local Apache-2.0 models, there are no minute allowances or subscription tiers.
