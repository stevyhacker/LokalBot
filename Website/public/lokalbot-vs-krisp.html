LokalBot vs Krisp

Krisp started as a best-in-class real-time noise cancellation app and expanded into a bot-free AI meeting assistant with transcription, notes, accent conversion, and CRM sync. Krisp's noise suppression runs on-device, while its meeting transcription, storage, and AI notes run in Krisp's cloud behind a subscription after a 7-day trial. LokalBot focuses on keeping the entire meeting pipeline on your computer for free under GPLv3, and the two apps can even run side by side if you rely on Krisp for background noise removal.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Krisp if…

Unmatched if your main pain point is noisy rooms or call-center audio.

  • You need real-time bidirectional background noise removal on calls
  • You want accent conversion, call-center manager views, or ConnectWise/Salesforce sync
  • You prefer hosted cloud call recordings with link sharing
  • Your company wants Enterprise SSO and centralized seat management

Pick LokalBot if…

Built for free, private meeting notes that never leave your hardware.

  • You want bot-free transcription and AI summaries without an $8-$30/mo subscription
  • Your call audio and transcripts must stay strictly on your own computer
  • You want unlimited local history with no 10 GB or 60 GB storage cap
  • You want Linux support, Obsidian Markdown export, dictation, autocomplete, and local MCP

Questions

The short answers.

Does LokalBot replace Krisp's noise cancellation?

No. LokalBot does not act as a live virtual microphone filter for removing barking dogs or café noise during a call. If you rely on Krisp for real-time noise removal, you can keep using noise cancellation (or macOS Voice Isolation) and let LokalBot handle private on-device transcription and notes.

Does Krisp still have a permanent free plan for AI notes?

Krisp's current pricing centers on a 7-day free trial followed by paid Core ($8/user/mo annual, $16 monthly) and Advanced ($15/user/mo annual, $30 monthly) plans. LokalBot is free with no trial countdown or storage caps.

Can I migrate Krisp transcripts to Obsidian?

Yes. You can convert Krisp text or JSON exports offline in your browser with the free Meeting Migrator into clean Obsidian Markdown with zero uploads.
