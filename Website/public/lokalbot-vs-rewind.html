LokalBot vs Rewind / Limitless

Rewind pioneered “search everything you've seen on your Mac” before rebranding to Limitless. After Meta acquired Limitless in December 2025, the Mac app disabled screen and audio capture on December 19, 2025, Limitless stopped selling its Pendant, and rewind.ai now hosts an unrelated site. LokalBot's day timeline carries the local-recall idea forward — smaller, opt-in, GPLv3 open source, and processed on your Mac, Windows, or Linux computer with no cloud backend.

Questions

The short answers.

Is LokalBot a drop-in Rewind replacement?

Not entirely, and it doesn't try to be. Rewind recorded your screen continuously and let you scrub it like a video. LokalBot tracks app and window activity by default, and adds periodic screenshots with OCR only if you opt in — lighter on disk, easier to reason about, and combined with full meeting capture. You get the search and the timeline, not the video scrubber.

What actually happened to Rewind?

The company behind it shifted to Limitless, a wearable pendant with cloud-hosted memory, and stopped developing the Mac app. Meta acquired Limitless in December 2025. A final update turned off Rewind's screen and audio capture on December 19, 2025, and Limitless stopped selling its Pendant. The rewind.ai domain now hosts an unrelated service — the original app is simply gone.

How is this different from Windows Recall?

Recall is Windows-only and drew heavy privacy scrutiny. LokalBot runs on the Mac and, in alpha, on Windows, where it's an open-source, opt-in alternative to Recall. Out of the box it logs only which app and window you had open; screen text and screenshots stay off until you turn them on. Screenshots are encrypted at rest, captures are deleted after 14 days by default, and the code that does all of that is open for you to audit.
