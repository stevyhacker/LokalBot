LokalBot vs Wispr Flow

Wispr Flow is a popular cloud dictation app across Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android, and its Notetaker records calls without a bot. Both features run in Wispr's cloud under a paid account. LokalBot is a free, open-source (GPLv3) alternative that runs dictation and bot-free meeting notes on your computer by default, adds a day timeline and 56 ms autocomplete, and natively imports your existing Wispr Flow meetings and personal dictionary directly from flow.sqlite with zero network calls.

Verdict

Which one fits you?

Pick Wispr Flow if…

The cloud dictation specialist, now with meeting notes.

  • Dictation is how you write most of the day, and you want AI cleanup, snippets, and per-app styles
  • You dictate on your phone too
  • You want meeting notes synced across devices in a managed cloud
  • Your company needs SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and SSO from a vendor

Pick LokalBot if…

Open-source local dictation and meeting notes, with a one-click Wispr Flow import.

  • Your words and meetings cannot go to a third-party cloud (clients, patients, sources)
  • You want to keep your Wispr Flow meetings and custom dictionary via read-only flow.sqlite import
  • You want offline operation, no account, no word caps, plus a day timeline and autocomplete
  • You want GPLv3 source code you or your security team can audit

Questions

The short answers.

Can I import my Wispr Flow meetings and personal dictionary into LokalBot?

Yes. On Mac, Recording > Import Meetings From reads Wispr Flow's meetings table and transcript files from your local flow.sqlite database read-only, without touching dictation history, audio, or screenshots. Settings > Models > Transcribe > Advanced > Language & vocabulary > Import from Wispr Flow imports your custom dictionary words (skipping snippets and deleted entries) with zero network requests.

Does Wispr Flow have an offline or on-device mode?

No. Wispr's data-controls page states that transcription always happens in its cloud, and Notetaker transcripts are stored there for cross-device sync. LokalBot transcribes dictation and meetings on your computer after a one-time model download, and keeps working without a connection.

Is LokalBot's dictation as good as Wispr Flow's?

Test both on your own voice. Wispr Flow goes further on cloud prose editing, snippets, and per-app tone rewrites. LokalBot 0.14.0 ships fine-tuned Qwen3-ASR 1.7B (v3) with 100% custom vocabulary recall, 0 silence hallucinations, and automatic spacing/capitalization cleanup before pasting, all running offline on the same speech model used for meetings.

Can I use both?

Yes. They use different shortcuts, and LokalBot is free. Some people keep Wispr Flow for heavy phone dictation and use LokalBot for meetings they prefer not to upload, offline dictation, a searchable day timeline, and autocomplete.
