FAQ

Pricing sources and calculation notes

Where do the competitor subscription prices come from?

Every tier comes from each vendor's public pricing page checked between October 26 and November 20, 2026, matching the tables on LokalBot's comparison pages. You can switch between annual and monthly or Pro and Business tiers inside the calculator.

How is annual cloud audio upload size calculated?

When your stack includes at least one cloud-processed meeting or voice tool, we multiply weekly call and dictation hours by team seats and 50 working weeks per year, then estimate compressed audio upload at 64 kbps (28.8 MB per hour). Raw 16 kHz mono WAV audio is 4x larger at 115.2 MB per hour.

Why is LokalBot $0 per seat with 0 bytes uploaded?

LokalBot is GPLv3 open-source desktop software that runs Apache-2.0 transcription, summarization, search, and autocomplete models on your own Mac, Windows, or Linux computer. There are no LokalBot cloud inference servers to pay for and no user accounts.

Does this calculator send my numbers to a server?

No. All math, URL share links, and PNG receipt images run locally in your browser with zero analytics scripts, cookies, or network uploads.
