Getting each app's history out

Granola

Settings › Profile › Export historical data › Generate CSV.

No audio or line times.

Otter

Conversation › ⋯ › Export as TXT, DOCX or SRT, speaker names on. Bulk ZIP on Business.

PDF isn't read.

Fireflies

A meeting's Download icon (Pro or higher), or the account data export.

PDF and DOCX aren't read.

Fathom

Copy Transcript and Copy Summary into text files, plus Download Video.

No bulk export; copied dates lack a year.

Screenpipe

No export: quit Screenpipe, then choose ~/.screenpipe.

Text in compressed storage is skipped.

Zoom

Choose Documents/Zoom, or cloud Audio only + Audio transcript.

Cloud files have no topic; no notes.

Voice Memos

Drag recordings to Finder, or choose the Recordings folder in the panel.

Not tried on a real library; no speakers.

MacWhisper

.whisper files, exports, or main.sqlite for everything.

No "me" lines; split tracks lose audio.

Only read

Never changed, moved or deleted; databases open read-only.

No sign-in, no network

Nothing is downloaded. Closing the old account is up to you.

Nothing twice

Importing again, or a newer export, adds only what's new.

Their notes, kept

They become the summary, an untouched copy beside it. No auto-summary.

Mac only for now. Every reader is partial: none of these apps fully documents its export, so the readers follow help pages and open-source readers and were tested on made-up files. The menu, import panel and approval card haven't been checked in the running app yet.

Pictures are drawings with made-up content, not screenshots. Imported meetings get no screen memory; the log keeps counts only.
