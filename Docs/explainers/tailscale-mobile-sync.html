Sync your meetings to your phone with zero cloud

Your computer stays the source of truth. Paired phones and tablets hold an encrypted offline copy synced directly over local Wi-Fi or your Tailscale network, with no LokalBot server or relay. When a direct path is blocked, Tailscale may carry the already-encrypted traffic through its own relays.

How pairing works
1

Enable hosting on desktop

Open Settings › Phones & tablets. Nothing listens until you switch sharing on. Pick exact data classes and a 30, 90, or 365-day retention window.

2

Scan one-time QR code

Click Add a device to open a single-use offer (Noise_XXpsk3, 32-byte PSK expiring in 5 min). Scan with the app and approve the device on desktop.

3

Reconnect over LAN or Tailscale

Clients try Bonjour on Wi-Fi first, then the last working host, then saved Tailscale IPs and MagicDNS names with a 2-second timeout each. If Tailscale can't connect directly, it relays the Noise-encrypted bytes through its DERP servers, which can't read them.

Strict data boundary

Shared only when toggled on

  • Meetings & transcripts: titles, summaries, notes, actions, decisions, speaker display names.
  • Day Digests & Timeline: work sessions, app time per project, coding-agent sessions (no raw screen quotes).
  • People & Projects: display names, open actions, decisions, and project status notes.
  • Phone recordings: sent to desktop to transcribe, then removed from phone storage.

Never leaves your computer

  • Screen capture: screenshots, file paths, and captured screen text stay off phones.
  • Private stores: indexed Files folders, coding-agent notes, Gmail mail, and Personal Context.
  • Identity & secrets: attendee emails, voice profiles, speaker evidence, settings, and API keys.
  • Desktop Ask & Dream: local Ask chats and overnight Dream memory never sync.
Security properties

IP-gated listener

Drops non-private, non-Tailscale peers before any Noise byte. Caps active sockets at 64.

Non-syncable keys

Static X25519 keys stay in Apple Keychain (ThisDeviceOnly) and Android hardware Keystore.

Neutral Bonjour

Advertises LokalBot-XXXX from 4 hex chars of the key hash; can be turned off for Tailscale-only.

Revoke anytime

Remove a device in Settings to reject its key immediately. App Store (iOS 18+) & Google Play (Android 10+).
