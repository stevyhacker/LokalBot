LokalBot · Meeting briefs from Gmail · Mac

Know what's open in email
before every meeting

Connect Gmail with read-only access, and the Today page shows your recent conversations with the people you're about to meet, including who is still waiting on your reply. The mail is stored encrypted on your Mac, and the brief is written there too.

What you'll see

  • A brief for your next meeting that can mention an unanswered question or a date agreed in email.
  • A From email row with the latest new text of the most relevant conversation. Its subject, sender and age sit below it, plus waiting on your reply when the last message asks you something.
  • More context holds the other matches, up to five conversations in all.
  • Click a row to open the thread in Gmail, signed in as the account that holds it.

Conversations are matched to a meeting by its attendees' email addresses, never by name, and your own addresses never count as attendees.

Turn it on

  1. Open Settings → Personal Context → Email and click Add Gmail Account…
  2. Read LokalBot's notice about what it downloads, then click Continue.
  3. Pick your Google account. Google's consent screen asks for one permission, to view your email messages and settings. It's read-only: LokalBot can't send, delete or label mail.
  4. The first check starts right away. When it finishes, the account shows Checked … · N conversations. Add more accounts the same way.
Google's review is pending. Until Google verifies LokalBot's Gmail access, sign-in shows "Google hasn't verified this app". Choose Advanced, then Go to LokalBot, to continue. Until then, at most 100 people can connect.
  • No Google Cloud setup. LokalBot has its own Google client, and sign-in opens in a system sheet.
  • Each account has Check Now and Disconnect… in the same place.

From Gmail to your meeting card

  1. ConnectYou sign in, and Google grants read-only Gmail access.
  2. ChooseOnly conversations you wrote in, or with people on your calendar.
  3. SealEach conversation is saved encrypted on your Mac.
  4. MatchBefore a meeting, conversations with its attendees are found by address.
  5. BriefThey show on Today and inform the brief written on your Mac.

Which mail LokalBot downloads

It reads message headers first and downloads a conversation only when it qualifies, looking back 90 days.

Kept if either is true

  • You wrote in it. Gmail's Sent label decides, so a forged From header with your address doesn't count.
  • Someone on your calendar takes part and it isn't bulk mail. Calendar people are remembered for 90 days, so a meeting that has passed doesn't make its mail disappear.

Always skipped

  • Spam, Trash and Drafts.
  • Bulk mail: mailing lists and newsletters, automatic replies, no-reply and notification senders, and Gmail's Promotions, Social, Updates and Forums tabs.
  • What's inside attachments. Only their names are kept.

When it checks for new mail

At launch, at the first check after 6:00 each morning, about 30 minutes before a meeting, every 3 hours, and when you click Check Now. Never twice within 15 minutes. After the first full scan, LokalBot asks Gmail only for what changed since the last check.

How the conversations are picked

For your next meeting, LokalBot scores each downloaded conversation that includes an attendee and keeps the top five. The highest one is the From email row.

score = 100 × attendees in it
  + (90 − days since its last message)
  + 60 if it's waiting on your reply
  + 20 × meeting-title words in its subject

"Waiting on your reply" means the last message isn't yours, asks a question, and is under 30 days old.

Written on your Mac

  • A model on this Mac writes the brief: your Think model, when it's the built-in model, Apple Intelligence, or a server on this computer. It runs on its own before a meeting, or when you click Generate brief.
  • With a cloud Think model, the card shows a short brief put together without a model, such as "Ana Petrović is waiting on your reply about Budget dates." Mail is never sent to a cloud model.
  • Mail is evidence, not instructions. The model is told never to follow anything written inside an email.
  • Only your own lines are your words. Each line is labeled with who wrote it, so something another person asked for is never written up as your promise.

What stays on your Mac

Storage

Each conversation is its own file, sealed with AES-GCM. The key is in the macOS Keychain, and only your user account can read the files.

Sign-in

Google's tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain. Access tokens last an hour and renew on their own.

Network

Read requests, only to Google. LokalBot has no server, and mail is never sent to LokalBot or anyone else.

Retention

A rolling 90 days. Each check removes conversations that have left the window.

Logs

Counts only, such as how many conversations a check kept. Never addresses, subjects or message text.

Attachments

Their names only. LokalBot never downloads what's inside them.

Turning it off

  1. In Settings → Personal Context → Email, click Disconnect… next to the account and confirm.
  2. LokalBot deletes that account's mail and sign-in from your Mac first, then asks Google to revoke its access. You can check at myaccount.google.com/permissions, where LokalBot no longer appears.

Limits

Mac and Gmail only

The Windows and Linux apps don't have it yet, and neither IMAP nor Outlook accounts are supported.

Google's review

Pending. Until Google approves LokalBot's Gmail access, sign-in shows the unverified-app screen and at most 100 people can connect.

90 days of mail

Older conversations aren't downloaded, and the first check stops at 2,000 conversations and says so in Settings.

Briefs need a local model

With a cloud Think model you still get the From email rows and a short brief, but no model-written one.
