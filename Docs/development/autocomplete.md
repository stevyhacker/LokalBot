# Autocomplete (Cotyping engine)

Part of the [development guide](../../DEVELOPMENT.md).

- **Ghost text everywhere:** as you type in almost any macOS text field, a gray suggestion appears next to the cursor; press **Tab** to accept the next word or phrase (Settings → Autocomplete → **Each accept takes**), **`** to accept the rest, keep typing, or press **Esc** to dismiss. The subsystem remains named Cotyping in source and settings keys. Its Accessibility poll resolves the focused field and caret, a `CGEventTap` watches keystrokes, a borderless click-through `NSPanel` renders at the caret, and accepted text is inserted as synthetic Unicode keystrokes.
- **Flow while accepting:** a suggestion starts at up to four words (Settings → Autocomplete → **Suggestion length**). While it is accepted or typed through, `CotypingSuggestionExtension` tops it up once two words are left, so the ghost stays a few words ahead until the sentence ends; words already shown never change, and a sentence that ended is not continued. Tab takes a word without its trailing punctuation unless **Accept punctuation with the word** is on. Escape on a visible suggestion dismisses it without reaching the app and holds that field for ten seconds (**Escape on a suggestion** can send the key through; a composing input method always receives it). Search fields stay quiet. These follow a live comparison with Cotypist recorded in [Docs/guides/CotypingParityQA.md](../guides/CotypingParityQA.md).
- **Fields that report no caret:** some fields, such as Chrome's text areas and Qt Quick apps like Viber (whose text accessibility answers every character with an empty rect), answer every caret query with an empty box. With Screen Recording already granted, `CotypingVisualCaret` captures the field's frame (at the display's own scale, or three times its points on a non-Retina display), finds the line that ends at the caret with Vision's fast text recognition, and keeps only the caret's position: the line's end is aligned with the typed text, the font is sized from the typed text rather than the recognized one, and a caret bar read as "|" is taken as the caret. A caret line in Cyrillic, which fast recognition cannot read, uses accurate recognition (Russian and Ukrainian models, with the letters they lack such as ђ and љ excused) to decide which line it is, and `CotypingVisualCaretInk` measures that line's edges, baseline and caret bar from the capture's pixels, since accurate recognition gives only loose word boxes; a line over a picture rather than a flat background stays a popup. Typing along that line moves it by text width, measured again in the background every dozen characters (every four on a Cyrillic line) and found anew close to where the line wraps. Until the caret is found, nothing is shown rather than a popup beside the field; after three finds in a row fail in a field, that field gets the popup. Without Screen Recording, or in excluded apps and sites, the suggestion is shown as a popup just outside the field, never over its text.
- **Never over text:** a key that leaves the suggestion takes the ghost down at once, without waiting for the app to publish the key. Typed and accepted text moves the ghost by its width as the app draws it, spaces included, and once the app publishes the caret the ghost moves to it whenever it would cover typed text. Wrapped lines go only onto empty lines inside the field; a suggestion that does not fit is cut after the last word that does, and the session keeps only what is shown, so the accept keys never insert unseen words.
- **Its own dedicated on-device model:** Autocomplete decodes a dedicated model (recommended **Gemma 4 E2B Base**) **in-process via libllama** for low latency, with the localhost `llama-server` as the fallback. The prompt treats the model as a pure text continuer; a shared normalizer strips chat/`<think>` scaffolding, prompt echoes, and trailing-text duplication, then collapses the result to one line.
- **Or a server:** the Autocomplete picker in Settings → Models (On this Mac / Think's server / Another server, edited as a `CotypingServerDraft` and applied with Use model) can send completions to Think's server (with Think's model or another one there) or to any OpenAI-compatible server, hosted or on another device, including over Tailscale. `CotypingEngineSelector` then routes to `RemoteCotypingEngine` (`CotypingServerRoute.swift`), frees the on-device model and uses the model-server debounce. The automatic request type tries raw `/v1/completions` on self-hosted and generic servers and chat (`ChatContinuationEngine`, thinking off) on OpenAI, Anthropic and OpenRouter, and moves a server to chat after a 404/405/501 or a "not supported" answer. A chat reply cut off at its limit keeps its text; one that ran out inside reasoning the model cannot turn off is asked again with room for it, and that model keeps the room until LokalBot quits. Chat models often leave out the space before a new word ("at a" + "higher level"), so after a whole word a reply that starts with a letter or digit gets it back (`CotypingEngine.restoringWordSpace`); without it the seam guard would drop the suggestion as a misspelling. Each request takes an interactive slot on the origin's `RemoteInferenceGate`, is never replayed, and is abandoned after 15 s. `RemoteCotypingEngine.problem` keeps the last failure or empty reply until a suggestion arrives, and Writing shows it, since typing in other apps shows no error. The rehearsal checks `contextEpoch`, not the live `generation`, so keys typed into it do not drop a slow server's answer, and it says why an answer became no suggestion. A non-loopback origin needs its own autocomplete approval (`approvedRemoteAutocompleteOrigins`); plain HTTP is accepted only for private-network hosts (`InferenceEndpointPolicy.isPrivateNetwork`). Choosing a server is consent: the Writing context and learning toggles apply as set, so whatever they enable goes to the server with the text before the caret.
- **Completion quality:** **Gemma 4 E2B Base (Autocomplete)** is the first-launch default and the Balanced local preset's autocomplete model (3.85 GB, intended for 16 GB+ Macs). Existing saved selections are preserved. **LFM2.5 1.2B Instruct** remains available in the Lightweight local preset. Prompts retain paragraphs, lists and caret whitespace, keep up to 500 words within the existing 2,500-character window, and use concise subject/topic context. Native decoding enforces a typed space and ranks compatible tokens for rare word fragments; actual token counts bound the context window. Base completion models are excluded from the Main LLM picker.
- **Visible reply context:** the **Use visible text above the field** setting (on for new installs; saved settings without the key decode as off, so earlier installs keep theirs) captures up to three visible Accessibility messages/labels (420 characters) in the focused input's column, within 600 points above it. A bounded 70 ms/180-node traversal selects geometry before text; no pixels, OCR or whole document values are read. Shared capture exclusions, autocomplete exclusions, browser-origin checks and private-window titles (`ScreenContextPrivacy.isPrivateWindow`) apply before reading content. The context is checked against the current settings and exclusions when it is read and again on Tab (`CotypingVisibleContext.Policy.permits`); Tab never traverses the screen. Unsupported or timed-out reads fall back to ordinary completion. Context-grounded text does not enter local learning. The native model [visible-context replay](../../Benchmarks/Cotyping/README.md#visible-context-and-memory-replay) uses the same traversal on synthetic AX trees.
- **Whole-window text:** the **Use the focused window as context** setting (on for every install, including saved settings without the key; needs Screen Recording already granted) reads the focused window through ScreenCaptureKit and Vision `.accurate` with automatic language detection (`.fast` drops every accent and all Cyrillic). `CotypingWindowTextCapture` runs one read at a time off the keystroke path: the first focus read in a field starts it, later reads reuse it and start a background refresh at most every 4 s; capture plus recognition measured 150–250 ms on a full window. The Accessibility walk for the privacy target (64 levels, no pane required) runs inside that read, so a keystroke only reads the field frame and window title for the key (`CotypingWindowText.key`: process, role, window title, field column). `CotypingWindowText.excerpts` drops lines inside the field, credential and instruction lines, then keeps 2,000 characters: the field's column above it nearest-first, then the rest of the window in reading order, placed before the conversation. A reread replaces the text in use only when it adds 12+ new characters, the title or URL changes, or the text is a minute old (`shouldReplace`), so the prompt start stays byte-identical and llama reuses its KV cache; a replacement triggers a background prefill. The prompt block (`Visible in this window:`) has its own budget ahead of the 900-character preface, and the autocomplete context is 4,096 tokens so a long draft plus a full window never truncates the prompt front. Window text replaces the Accessibility excerpts once present; it is held per field, forgotten with focus or policy changes, and excluded from learning like nearby text.
- **Relevant saved facts:** two independent, default-off settings allow meeting/work memory and screen-derived work memory. A local lookup selects at most two 180-character snippets, limited to the last 90 days, from current meeting notes, summaries, corrected outcomes, and attributed Dream projects/goals. The two grants are the whole permission to read saved work memory: they apply whether or not Overnight review is scheduled and never start a review; mixed provenance requires both source grants. FTS finds meeting candidates but never supplies prompt text. A fact qualifies only when its source is named (at least half of the title's distinctive words appear in the draft, window title, or selected visible text) or when it shares two distinctive words with the user's own draft or window title, one written as a name, or three when none is. Everyday wording, calendar words and app names (`CotypingMemoryContext.everydayTerms`) never count, a notes line that adds no word to its source's title (a heading) is dropped, and writing with no distinctive word returns before any file is opened. Visible text can name a source but never counts as the user's own wording. Settings shows each grant's effective state (available, unavailable and why, or nothing saved) and whether the last lookup found anything. Source edits/deletion or permission changes invalidate affected requests and cached suggestions; accepted memory-grounded suggestions are excluded from the learning store. Setup samples never retrieve private context. See the synthetic [memory replay](../../Benchmarks/Cotyping/README.md#saved-memory-replay), which scores ignoring irrelevant memory alongside recall.
- **Opt-in & private:** off by default; needs **Accessibility** + **Input Monitoring**. Never reads password/secure fields; honors a per-user app exclusion list (preseeded with password managers and terminals).
- **In-app preview:** Settings → Autocomplete → **Try it…** opens a rehearsal that runs the real prompt, model and normalizer before the feature is enabled system-wide; readiness is the page header's status lines. The rehearsal and the live accept tap share `CotypingContinuationAcceptance`, so a rehearsed Tab takes the configured word or phrase with the same spacing and punctuation, the full-accept key takes the rest, typing the suggested characters walks through the ghost, and moving the caret or typing anything else discards it. A synthetic sample conversation above the reply field stands in for nearby text when that grant is on (it goes through the same bounded selection; the screen is never read), and each optional source is listed as used, found nothing relevant, off, or unavailable. The menu bar provides the quick toggle.
- **Typing measurements:** generation latency alone leaves out the wait for the host to publish a keystroke, the pause before suggesting, context lookup, validation and the overlay. `CotypingLiveMeasure` records, per kind of app, the time from the last keystroke to a visible suggestion, whether an accepted suggestion then appeared in the field (`CotypingInsertionCheck` compares the published text with what was sent), and whether it was deleted or undone at once. Only the whole inserted text counts as arrived, and a check closes on its own after 1.5 s, so an app that ignores an insertion is counted and the compared text is not kept. Counts and timings only. Settings → Autocomplete → **Usage and timing** shows, copies and resets them; `--cotyping-measurements` prints them for scripts.
- **Statistics:** `CotypingUsageHistory` keeps per-day (92 days) and per-month counts, each split by kind of app and model, under `lokalbotv3.cotypingUsage`. A suggestion counts as shown each time a continuation is presented and as used at its first accept, so the share used never passes 100%; typing counts only where `CotypingAvailability` would suggest, and gaps of up to 1 s between typed characters give the typing speed that prices saved keystrokes (characters accepted minus accept presses; 40 words a minute until 200 gaps are measured). Typed characters update the store without publishing or writing; the next generation, completed suggestion or quit writes them. Settings → Autocomplete → **Statistics** (`AutocompleteStatisticsView`) charts words or share used by day or month and breaks them down by kind of app and, once there are two, by model.
