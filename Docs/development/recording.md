# Recording & meeting detection

Part of the [development guide](../../DEVELOPMENT.md).

- **Detection:** native meeting apps combine process audio signals with bounded start/stop policies. `MeetingDetector.knownApps` lists them. Zoom, Webex, Tencent Meeting, and VooV Meeting are dedicated conferencing apps whose audio starts a recording at once; Teams, Slack, FaceTime, Feishu, Lark, DingTalk, and WeCom also carry chat sounds and voice messages, so their audio must last `nativeAudioMinimumConfirmationDuration` unless a calendar event backs it. WeChat is deliberately not listed, because its voice messages would pass that gate. Call audio from helper processes counts when the helper sits in the host's bundle namespace, plus the three that do not: Feishu's `com.bytedance.lark.helper`, DingTalk's `com.alibaba.DingTalk-Helper`, and Webex's `Cisco-Systems.SparkHelper` (`MeetingDetector.foreignHelperHosts`). Browser automatic starts require supported Google Meet in-call controls across consecutive observations; calendar entries and arbitrary browser output never authorize a start. Once authorized, an unreadable document, missing/unsupported controls, or a missing browser host does not stop or trim capture. Only explicit leave/end evidence, continuously verified bound-call closure after grace, a verified call in another room, or user Stop ends that browser recording. Meet's post-call page counts as leave evidence by its Rejoin and Return to home screen actions, whatever its heading says. Closure checks inspect other browser windows so moving the tab does not itself end the call. The original call binding survives uncertainty; a replacement browser host must verify the same room before capture attaches. While the bound call is unverified, the detector also reads other rooms (only the active calendar event's room when it has one); a call there that passes the normal start gate ends the stale session without trimming it and starts its own. While the bound call still holds its window, the next calendar event on the same room link splits the recording, as it does for native apps; an event in another room means the call is running over. A manual Start joins the detector's session only while its call is verified, and never takes an unverified call's room. A persistent notice explains that recording continues and browser process audio can include other tabs. Call observation diagnostics stay separate from content boundaries. The live capture environment reads each running app's pid, bundle id, and name once per process (`RunningApplicationCache`) instead of through LaunchServices on every 2-second detector tick, and `AudioSourceMonitor` queries Core Audio's process list on a utility thread. When a calendar-backed call ends on its own, rejoining the same event records again right away; after the user presses Stop, automatic detection waits five minutes before recording that event again. The next calendar event in a continuing call is a new meeting and records automatically even after the user stopped the previous one.
- **Two synchronized tracks:** `mic.m4a` (AVAudioEngine) captures microphone input; `system.m4a` captures the selected application process through a Core Audio tap. Audio source is separate from speaker identity. The engine re-installs its tap on `AVAudioEngineConfigurationChange` (AirPods/USB switches no longer truncate the recording), drains the converter on stop (keeps trailing audio), recovers helper-process changes without ending the microphone track, and encodes AAC off the real-time IOProc thread. A microphone that cannot be rebuilt within its fast retries (about ten seconds) is retried every 30 seconds for the rest of the recording, like system audio, instead of staying silent.
- **Recording timeline:** new recordings use timing version 2 and one continuous origin for both tracks and preview files. Late attachment, recovered gaps, and sleep insert bounded silence, preserving playback/transcript alignment. Detector lifecycle IDs prevent an unrelated native meeting-end event from stopping a manual recording. Historical version-1 recordings retain their original interpretation; repairing them would require a coordinated audio, transcript, and speaker-evidence migration.
- **Recovery and health:** meeting-intent recording persists its requested app/room and keeps late attachment alive for the recording's lifetime (fast initial retries, then every 30 seconds). Verified detector events wake it immediately; microphone-only intent never acquires a system source. Generation checks reject delayed work after Stop. The one-second watchdog reports missing successful writes at a five-second threshold, treats flowing silence separately, and checks disk space every 15 seconds. Persistent warnings and the menu-bar indicator supplement the per-source status. A warning becomes a one-time notification only once it persists (`CaptureWarningNotifications`): missing audio after 15 s, lost call status after the detector's 120-second grace, disk and write failures at once; dropped buffers never notify. `recording-health.json` records state transitions, dropped buffers, attempts, last verified observation, and completion. Capture warnings remain visible beside completed transcripts and notes; fewer than five dropped buffers per source do not count as a capture issue. Both recorders hand tap buffers between the real-time thread and the writer queue through a lock-free ring (`RealtimeBufferRing`), so a buffer is dropped only when all of them are in use. Browser observation enables a browser's page accessibility once per process: `AXManualAccessibility` for Chromium, `AXEnhancedUserInterface` only for Firefox, nothing for Safari.
- **Independent audio persistence:** both recorders attempt PCM recovery independently of AAC writes. Each track has a growing 16 kHz mono CAF plus independently closed PCM checkpoints under `mic.live.recovery/` or `system.live.recovery/`. Checkpoints target two seconds at buffer boundaries, close and synchronize before a SHA-256/frame-offset manifest is atomically saved, and consume about 230 MB/hour per PCM copy per track. Recovery reconstructs contiguous verified audio, can read an unmanifested final chunk, and retains original chunks on any coverage uncertainty. It never replaces a longer verified reconstruction with a shorter one. Redundant copies are removed only after complete primary decoding and duration/coverage checks (100 ms encoder tolerance). Relaunch repairs existing media without authorizing new capture. This is tested process-crash recovery, not a power-loss durability guarantee.
- **Capture priority:** new post-meeting jobs wait while recording starts or runs; active work yields at track/model phase boundaries after saving completed work. Recording no longer prewarms inference models. An in-flight model call is not forcibly preempted. Optional live transcription retains its separate opt-in.
- **Finalization:** stopping preserves failed metadata/processing-queue writes for retry and surfaces the error. Quitting saves automatic processing intent durably for the next launch without starting inference; an unsaved finalization cancels quitting so the storage error can be resolved and Quit retried. Reconnecting a microphone rearms bounded recovery after its earlier attempts were exhausted.
- **Missing transcripts:** after launch recovery, finished recordings of at least 5 seconds with readable audio, no transcript, and no queued, parked, or running job are queued once with automatic processing settings. A merged meeting whose transcript has no segment in the span of a source of at least 30 seconds is re-transcribed once; `transcript-gap-repair.json` records the attempt.
- **Imported recordings:** dropping an audio or video file onto Meetings or the welcome card, or choosing Recording → Import Recording…, runs `MeetingImportService`. It mixes the file's enabled audio tracks to AAC beside the library, moves the result into a reserved meeting folder as `system.m4a`, and writes `meta.json` last, so a library scan never sees a half-made meeting. The meeting is dated from the container's creation date, else the earlier of the file's creation date and modification time minus its duration, and never ends in the future. It records `importedFileNames`, which a merge carries over from its sources so the merged meeting stays imported, and then takes the normal user-initiated pipeline path. With no microphone track, its speakers are diarized system speakers, remembered and matched like call participants, and screen context from its time span is not attached.
- **Recordings from paired devices:** a phone or tablet sends an ADTS AAC file over the companion session (`Companion/protocol/protocol.md`, `purpose: "upload"`). `lokalbot-sync` resumes partial files in `companion/uploads/` and checks the SHA-256, then `CompanionRecordingReceiver` calls `MeetingImportService.importDeviceRecording`. The meeting takes the device's id, so a resend finds it, and its audio is `mic.m4a`, because a phone's microphone is the person's own. `importedFileNames` keeps screen context away. The pipeline starts without opening anything, and the device deletes its copy once it hears `accepted`, so that answer waits until the meeting is stored.
- **Menu bar:** the status item opens a panel (`MenuBarExtra`, window style; `MenuBarView`), shaped like a system status menu: one status block, then rows, each with an icon. `MenuBarStatus` picks the block's one state, in this order: recording (red: timer, the meeting, whether the microphone and the call's audio are arriving, Stop and Open live notes; amber while a capture warning stands), a running dictation (what it is doing, with Stop or Cancel), needs attention (amber: microphone access, the latest error, a settings change waiting for approval, each with its fix), a calendar meeting starting within `MenuBarStatus.soonWindow` or under way and not recorded (teal: Join and Record; the same schedule and rule as Today, read through `UpcomingMeetingSchedule` only while the panel is open, and never without the Calendar setting and macOS access), day memory paused, a recording saved within `MenuBarStatus.justStoppedWindow` (its name, length and Open notes), or not recording. A recording or a dictation keeps the block when something else needs attention, so Stop is never hidden; those items are listed under it. The rows are Quick Recall, Start dictation (it stops or cancels a running dictation and waits while a meeting records), Copy last dictation (earlier ones in its context menu), the two latest meetings, the Autocomplete switch, Pause day memory, Open LokalBot, Settings… and Quit; Live Radar and Hand off this window appear when those features can be used. ⌘, and ⌘Q are bound in the panel as well as in the app menu. The status item's glyph (`MenuBarIcon`, template images) shows the recording dot and timer, the dictation states, dims while day memory is paused and carries a dot while something needs attention; `AppSceneState` publishes those two states so the item redraws only when its glyph changes. The clock, the calendar and the sound levels are read only while the panel's window is on screen (`WindowVisibilityReader`).
- **UI:** a live meeting opens with quick notes and an opt-in rolling transcript. A completed meeting opens as a workspace with synchronized playback, reviewable action items, decisions, summary, transcript evidence, export, and reprocessing controls.
