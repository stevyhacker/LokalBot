LokalBot brings your private meeting recorder and work memory to iPhone and iPad, with no account, no cloud server, and no third-party tracking.

RECORD MEETINGS RIGHT AWAY
Open the app and start recording an in-person meeting or voice memo immediately, even before pairing with a computer.
- Continues recording cleanly with the screen locked or while switching apps.
- Writes directly to a crash-safe AAC stream inside private app storage excluded from iCloud backups.
- Makes zero network requests while unpaired. Review, rename, or delete saved recordings on your device anytime.
- Pair with LokalBot on Mac, Windows, or Linux whenever you are ready. Held recordings transfer straight to your computer over an end-to-end encrypted connection, get transcribed and summarized locally on your desktop, and leave your phone once your computer confirms receipt.

DIRECT, END-TO-END ENCRYPTED DESKTOP SYNC
Your computer stays the source of truth. Pair by scanning a one-time QR code in LokalBot on macOS, Windows, or Linux and approving the device on your computer.
- Syncs directly over your local Wi-Fi network or from anywhere over Tailscale.
- Every connection uses mutual authentication and Noise Protocol encryption (X25519, ChaChaPoly, BLAKE2s).
- LokalBot operates no sync server, relay, or cloud database.
- You choose on your computer which data classes and how many days of history to share.

YOUR WORK MEMORY, READABLE OFFLINE
- Today and Day Digests: see overdue actions, today's meetings, time tracked, and your latest digest even on a flight.
- Meetings and Transcripts: read summaries, your notes, decisions, open questions, and timestamped speaker transcripts.
- Actions: filter by mine, others, or unclear owner, jump straight to the transcript evidence, and mark items done or deferred offline. Changes sync back on the next connection.
- Timeline, People, and Projects: browse work sessions, coding-agent milestones, shared history with each teammate, and where tracked projects stand.
- Ask with Citations: query your desktop's Ask engine from your phone, jump directly to cited meeting moments, queue questions when your computer is asleep, and share answers complete with their cited meeting titles.

TRY BEFORE YOU PAIR
Tap Try It Without a Computer to explore a built-in sample library right on your device, or start recording a real meeting immediately.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
- Zero accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, or telemetry.
- Optional Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode App Lock that hides your library in the app switcher.
- Local copies and saved recordings stay out of device backups.
- Open-source desktop and mobile companion code you can inspect at github.com/stevyhacker/LokalBot.
