No account or login is required.

To test recording immediately without pairing a computer:
1. On the welcome screen, tap "Record a Meeting" and grant microphone permission.
2. Record a short audio clip, optionally enter a title, and tap "Stop and Save".
3. Confirm the saved recording appears in the on-device recordings card on the welcome screen with its timestamp, duration, and delete button.
4. Background recording (`UIBackgroundModes: audio`) keeps the active recording running when the screen locks or the user switches apps, and shows the system microphone indicator.

To test the full work-memory experience without a desktop computer:
1. On the welcome screen, tap "Try It Without a Computer".
2. This loads a built-in sample library (Today, Meetings, Actions, Timeline, People, Projects, and Ask with sample answers and clickable citations).
3. You can also record from the microphone button in Meetings or Today while in sample mode; recordings remain strictly in private on-device storage with zero network traffic until a computer is paired.

Desktop pairing and permissions:
- The companion pairs directly with the open-source LokalBot desktop app (macOS, Windows, or Linux) over the user's local Wi-Fi network (Bonjour `_lokalbot._tcp`) or Tailscale, end-to-end encrypted with Noise (X25519, ChaChaPoly, BLAKE2s).
- Camera permission is used solely to scan the one-time pairing QR code displayed in LokalBot on the desktop.
- Local Network permission is used solely to discover the paired computer on the user's LAN.
- Face ID / Touch ID is used solely when the user enables optional App Lock in Settings.
- No data is sent to any LokalBot server.
